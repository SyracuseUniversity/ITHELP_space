Follow the steps below to download a copy of Windows 11.

## Downloading Windows 11

### Visit Azure Dev Tools site

1. Navigate to <https://azureforeducation.microsoft.com/devtools/> and click the blue "Sign In" button.

   ![azure sign in.jpg](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941080/azure%20sign%20in.jpg?api=v2)

### Login with your SU account and sign up

1. Log in with your SU provided Microsoft account, using your [NetID@syr.edu](mailto:NetID@syr.edu) email and NetID password. If prompted, make sure to select your Work or School Account, and complete MFA verification.
2. Complete the sign up process, verifying your name, address, school and other information.

   ![azure sign up form.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941080/azure%20sign%20up%20form.png?api=v2)

### Education Hub Redirect

1. You will then be redirected to the Microsoft Azure [Education Hub](https://portal.azure.com/#blade/Microsoft_Azure_Education/EducationMenuBlade/software)

   ![education hub.jpg](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941080/education%20hub.jpg?api=v2)

### Software Tab

1. Click on the "Software" tab in the sidebar to view and download available software.
2. Choose the Window option you are looking to download. There may be multiple versions of Windows available for download,

   ![software tab.jpg](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941080/software%20tab.jpg?api=v2)

## Need Help?

1. For more information, please see [Microsoft Azure Dev Tools for Teaching: Download instructions for students](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/education-hub/azure-dev-tools-teaching/download-software).

**Free Installation for Students**

Any student needing assistance with the installation of Windows 11 on their device is welcome to bring the device to the ITS Service Center [during business hours](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/). Consultants can perform any software related task, Windows 11 installation or otherwise, free of charge. Just bring your SUID card, the device, and the power adapter. Note that Windows 11, or other operating system installation, can take a significant amount of time and may require that you leave your device at the ITS Service Center for your request to be completed.
