# About GET

GET is a place where students (and parents/guardians) can manage the Cuse Cash funds, find places to eat and more. GET provides valuable information about account balances and spending history, and enables you to report a lost or stolen card at any time of day or night.

This option requires an immediate payment but offers parents and relatives the opportunity to add funds to their student’s account for immediate use.

Parents or other relatives wishing to add funds to their student’s account may do so using GET.  Please click [GET instructions for Parents](http://housingmealplans.syr.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Getinstr.pdf) to review instructions on how to add funds.

### Summer Information

Students are unable to add funds during the summer hours using GET. Students are able to add funds to their accounts in person during the summer by stopping into 111 Waverly Avenue Suite 111 during university business hours

# Accessing GET

## Accessing GET in a Browser

GET is available via a web based interface for Windows and Mac computer users.

To log in, open your browser of choice and visit <https://get.cbord.com/portal/full/portal.php>.

Select Syracuse University in the drop down menu.

![GET university selection screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/Website%20-%20Select%20Institution%20drop%20down.jpg?api=v2)

You'll now be taken to the Syracuse University GET options page.

![A picture of the SU GET options page is shown.](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/get_options.png?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940765/GET+-+Managing+Your+Campus+Money+Food+More#top)

### Student Web Login

Students should select the Click here to login link.

![A picture of the GET login page is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/student_get_login.png?api=v2)

You should be taken to a Syracuse University login page. Enter your [YourNetID@syr.edu](mailto:NetID@syr.edu) as the username and your NetID password. 

![SU Microsoft sign in page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/Screenshot%202025-07-30%20102606.png?api=v2)

Once logged in, you'll be taken to your account home page. 

![A screenshot of the student account page is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/student_account_home.png?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940765/GET+-+Managing+Your+Campus+Money+Food+More#top)

### Parent/Guardian Web Login

Parents or guardians will want to select the [Click Here to Deposit into a Student's Account link](https://get.cbord.com/syracuse/full/guest_deposit.php). There is no parent account creation required. Your parent/guardian MySlice proxy account credentials **will not** allow access to the GET log in location.

![A screenshot of the GET login page with instructions for parents](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/parent_get_login.png?api=v2)

### $25 Minimum Deposit

Note that there is a $25 minimum for depositing funds through GET.

Next, enter the students information including SUID, first name, and last name. All three fields are required to add funds.

![A screenshot of the required fields to enter for the student](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/get_student_info.png?api=v2)

### SUID Provided by Student

Syracuse University representatives are not authorized to provide parents or guardians with student SUIDs. Please contact the student to be provide this information.

Once you have selected the correct student, follow the prompts on the screen to add credit card information, confirm your purchase, and finalize the transaction. The funds should become available to the student immediately.

## Accessing GET on a Mobile Device

GET is available on iOS and Android devices. To download, open the corresponding app store and search GET or click on the icons below.

[![Google play icon](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/1280px-Get_it_on_Google_play.svg.png?api=v2)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cbord.get)[![Apple app store icon](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/applestore-btn.png?api=v2)](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/get-mobile/id844091049?mt=8)

You’ll also find a link to GET in the Syracuse University Mobile App under the “Dining” icon.

### First Time Setup

Once downloaded, open the application. Locate Syracuse University and select it to continue.

![Mobile app school selection screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/Mobile%20-First%20Time%20Setup.PNG?api=v2)

You'll be taken to a Syracuse University login screen. Enter [YourNetID@syr.edu](mailto:YourNetID@syr.edu) as the username and your NetID password.

![SU Microsoft sign in page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/Screenshot%202025-07-30%20102606.png?api=v2)

Next, update your contact information and create a PIN for your app account.

![Pin creation screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/Mobile%20-%20Pin%20Creation.PNG?api=v2)

### Notate Your PIN

If you've misplaced it or it no longer functions, please contact the housing, meal plan, and ID card services office by calling 315.443.2721, emailing [housing@syr.edu](mailto:housing@syr.edu), or stopping into 111 Waverly Avenue, Suite 111 during university business hours.

You'll now be taken to the home page. For future logins you may be required to authenticate the account using a fingerprint or the PIN you set up for your GET account.

![Mobile app funds screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940765/Mobile%20-Home%20Page%20-%20Add%20Funds.png?api=v2)

# Getting Help

## Login Password Help

The GET app will look for your NetID and password once you have selected Syracuse University. This username is managed on the [NetID self-service page](https://netid.syr.edu/selfserv/home). There you can use the "Additional Services" option to test your login credentials.

If you are continuing to have trouble logging into the application you may contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc) by calling at 315.443.2677, by emailing [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu)

## Login PIN, Transaction and Balance Help

If you are having trouble with managing your funds or would like to report discrepancies with the information found within the app, please contact the housing, meal plan, and ID card services office by calling 315.443.2721, emailing [housing@syr.edu](mailto:housing@syr.edu), or stopping into 111 Waverly Avenue, Suite 111 during university business hours.
