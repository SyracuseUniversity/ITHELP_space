There are two situations, depending on whether you still have your old phone.

- **You still have your old phone and it works:** Follow Part 1 to move your Authenticator app to the new phone yourself, before you stop using the old one.
- **You no longer have your old phone, or the app isn’t working:** Skip to Part 2.

### Part 1: Set up your new phone while you still have your old one

Don’t erase, trade in, or reset your old phone until you’ve finished these steps and confirmed the new phone works.

1. On your **new phone**, install the **Microsoft Authenticator** app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). Don’t sign in yet.
2. On a **computer**, go to <https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info> and sign in with your Syracuse University email address and NetID password.
3. When prompted for MFA, approve the request using the Authenticator app on your **old phone**.
4. On the Security info page, select **Add sign-in method**, then choose **Authenticator app** (or **Microsoft Authenticator**) and select **Add**.
5. Select **Next** until a **QR code** appears on the screen. Keep this page open.
6. On your **new phone**, open the Authenticator app, select **+** (Add account), choose **Work or school account**, and select **Scan a QR code**. Scan the code on your computer screen.
7. Back on the computer, select **Next**. Approve the test notification on your **new phone** (enter the number shown on the computer screen if asked).
8. Confirm the new phone appears in your list of sign-in methods on the Security info page.
9. Remove the old phone: on the Security info page, find the old **Microsoft Authenticator** entry (it shows your old device name) and select **Delete**. Then remove the Syracuse University account from the Authenticator app on the old phone.
10. Test it by signing in to a Syracuse University service, such as SUMail or MySlice, and approve the request on your new phone.

**Tip:** If you have more than one Authenticator entry listed and aren’t sure which is the old phone, delete only the one you can no longer approve requests from.

### Part 2: What to do if you don’t have your old phone

For **students**: whenever you get a new phone, you will need to contact us to reset the Authenticator app. Please call us at 315-443-2677 as we will need to verify your identity to get the app set up on your new phone. If you are unable to call us, you can send an email to [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) with your preferred day and time in Eastern Time for a Zoom appointment.

For **parent/proxy users:** whenever you get a new phone, **your student** will need to contact us at 315-443-2677 so we can verify their identity, after which we will reset the Authenticator app for the proxy account.

**Staff** must complete additional verification to reset their Authenticator app. They can do this by contacting us at 315-443-2677 and completing a Zoom verification meeting, or by visiting us in-person at the Service Center in the lobby of the Women’s Building.

**Faculty** needing the Authenticator app reset must contact their [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel.
