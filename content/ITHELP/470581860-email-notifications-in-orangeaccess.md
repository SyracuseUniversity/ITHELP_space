OrangeAccess sends automated email notifications to keep you informed about your access requests. Notifications are sent when you submit a request, when it is approved or denied, and when access is removed.

---

## Who Sends the Emails?

All OrangeAccess notifications come from:

**Orange Access** [**orangeaccess@syr.edu**](mailto:orangeaccess@syr.edu)

Add this address to your **Safe Senders** list to ensure you always receive these emails.

---

## What Types of Emails Will I Receive?

- **Request submitted** – Confirmation that your request was received.
- **Approval needed** – Sent to managers or application/data owners when a request requires their review.
- **Request approved or denied** – Updates on the outcome of your request.
- **Access removed** – Confirmation when access has been revoked.

## Viewing Images in Outlook/Exchange

By default, Outlook does not display images in messages from new senders. When you first receive an email from OrangeAccess, you may see an informational banner at the top:

> *“Click here to download pictures…”*

To ensure emails display correctly in the future:

1. Click **Download Pictures**.
2. Select **Add Sender to Safe Senders List**.

📸 *Screenshot:* ![Outlook banner with 'Click here to download pictures...' highlighted](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/470581860/OA-Email1.png?api=v2)

---

✅ **Tip:** Adding [*orangeaccess@syr.edu*](mailto:orangeaccess@syr.edu) to your Safe Senders list not only displays images automatically but also helps prevent OrangeAccess emails from being sent to Junk/Spam.
