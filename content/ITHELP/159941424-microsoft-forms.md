## About Microsoft Forms at Syracuse University

#### What does Forms provide?

Microsoft Forms can be used to create surveys, quizzes, and polls, and easily see results as they come in.

Form data, such as quiz results, can be easily exported to Excel for additional analysis or grading.

#### What to know about Forms at Syracuse.

- Forms can be shared and restricted to responses only from users at SU, or from anyone with the link.
