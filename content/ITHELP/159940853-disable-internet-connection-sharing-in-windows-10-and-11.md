# Step 1: Open the Control Panel

Hold down the Windows key (![Windows icon](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/icon.PNG?api=v2)) and press "R", then type "control panel" or "control" (minutes the quotations).

![image of the run box searching for control panel](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/Run.controlpanel%20pic.png?api=v2)

# Step 2: Locate Your Network Connections

 In Control Panel, look for “View by” on the top-right corner and change it to “Large icons”. Then, click on "Network and Sharing Center".

![A screenshot of the control panel](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/network%20and%20sharing.png?api=v2)

Now in "Network and Sharing Center" select "Change Adapter Settings". This will take you to your "Network Connection".

![Windows network and sharing window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/selecting.PNG?api=v2)

# Step 3: Open the Properties of Your Wi-Fi Adapter

 Right click the adapter that is connected (or going to be connected) to AirOrangeX and select "Properties".

![Wifi properties](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/properties.PNG?api=v2)

# Step 3: Disable Connection Sharing

In the adapter's Wi-Fi Properties select the "Sharing" tab.

![Wifi properties access window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/props.PNG?api=v2)

Be sure that the check box indicated is **NOT** check and press "OK".

![Wi-fi properties window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940853/sharingticker.PNG?api=v2)

You have now disabled internet connection sharing in Windows 10 or 11.
