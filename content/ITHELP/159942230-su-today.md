Syracuse University allows user to subscribe to regular emails including the latest from [SU News](http://news.syr.edu).

---

## Subscribing and Unsubscribing

To subscribe to SU Today, visit [http://news.syr.edu/subscribe/](http://news.syr.edu/subscribe/ "Follow link"), enter your email address into the field that is just above the Subscribe button (note that the box for the field may not be visible, you can tab into it, or just place your cursor in that space), then click the Subscribe button.   You can also send a request to [sunews@syr.edu](mailto:sunews@syr.edu "Follow link").

To unsubscribe from SU Today, send a request to [sunews@syr.edu](mailto:sunews@syr.edu "Follow link"),  or send an email (from the account that receives SU Today) to listserv@listserv.syr.edu with a blank subject line and the following as the first and only line of text in the email:

 unsubscribe SUTODAY

\*News web sites\*

If you wish to submit news: <https://news.syr.edu/submit/>
 To search for past news items: [SU News](http://www.syr.edu/news/)

---

## Getting Help

If you need help with your subscription, contact [sunews@syr.edu](mailto:sunews@syr.edu).
