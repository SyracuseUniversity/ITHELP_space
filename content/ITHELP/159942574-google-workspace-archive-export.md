There is no longer the ability to retrieve Google Workspace (G Suite) Drive data, for users that were effected by the December 2022 account deletion due to a change in terms of agreement. Users effected by this change include, but are not limited to, active alumni, all inactive affiliations and SU Guest active accounts (that are ineligible for continued access).  The archive was removed after 6 months of availability (on June 28, 2023).  The data is no longer recoverable.

If any further support is needed with this process feel free to contact the Help Desk directly at 315.443.2677 or email [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu)
