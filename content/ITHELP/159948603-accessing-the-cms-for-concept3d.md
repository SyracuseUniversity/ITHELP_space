Most users will access the CMS for Concept3D using their NetID and password through the [NetID SSO Login](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159948603/Accessing+the+CMS+for+Concept3D#AccessingtheCMSforConcept3D-NetIDSSOLogin) process outlined below. There is also a separate URL if you have a Local Administrator and/or Test account, which requires a different login URL.

In order to access the CMS for Concept3D, you must have an account created by the application administrators.

## NetID SSO Login

---

1. Navigate to the following URL: [https://mapadmin.syracuse.edu](https://mapadmin.syracuse.edu/)
2. Enter your NetID and password in the Shibboleth login screen

The Shibboleth login can also be accessed at: <https://cms.concept3d.com/map/login/checklogin_shibboleth.php?idp=shibidp.syr.edu>

## Local Administrator and Test Login

---

Some users have a Local Administrator and/or a Test account. These accounts can not be logged into through the Shibboleth SSO, as authentication is through Concept3D

1. Navigate to the following URL: [https://cms.concept3d.com](https://cms.concept3d.com/?id=1275)
2. Enter your credentials
