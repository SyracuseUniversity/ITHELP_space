## Before You Log In: Activate Your NetID

---

Access to MySlice, like many of the University's computing services, is controlled through your NetID username and password. Before you will be able to sign into your MySlice portal, you must first activate your NetID. To do so, navigate to the [NetID Self-Serv](https://netid.syr.edu/selfserv/) page, click **Activate your NetID**, and follow the prompts to activate.

Note that it can take up to an hour after activating your NetID before you will be able to login to MySlice.

If you need assistance with the activation process please contact the help desk at [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) or 315-443-2677

**NetID Activation will require your 9-digit SUID number.**

Your NetID is not the same as your SUID. Your NetID is your username (for example, *otto44*), while your SUID is the 9-digit number you'll use to activate it.

**Applicants** will find their SUID number in the email sent from Syracuse University confirming receipt of application. If you did not receive this email (be sure to check your spam folder), please write to Admissions at[orange@syr.edu](mailto:orange@syr.edu).

**Project Advance Students** will find their SUID in the welcome email sent from Syracuse University Project Advance after officially registering for a SUPA course.

If you need further assistance with your SUID, please contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc).

#### **Have a Temporary NetID?**

**Students in the College of Professional Studies** who are auditing classes or taking classes as non-matriculated students may first receive a temporary NetID and password. These temporary credentials are then used to obtain your SUID, before activating the NetID.

ITS Help Staff do not have a way of verifying or viewing temporary credentials. Questions or issues about temporary NetID’s should be referred to the [Bursar & Registration Office (CPS)](https://professionalstudies.syracuse.edu/about/office-directory/bursar-registration-office/)

Additional information is available on the [Accounts and NetIDs](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/RIGICQ) page.

## Instructions for Accessing MySlice as an Applicant or SUPA (Project Advance) Instructor or Student

### Step 1: Applicants: Activate and Manage Your NetID and Password

- Once you have received your SUID number (which is in the email sent from Syracuse University confirming receipt of application) activate your NetID by going to NetID SelfServ (at [netid.syr.edu](https://netid.syr.edu)) and selecting **Activate your NetID** from the options provided.
- Follow the on screen instructions to finish your NetID activation.

Note that it can take up to an hour after activating your NetID before you will be able to login to MySlice.

### Step 2: Navigate to MySlice and Log In

- Using a [supported MySlice browser](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/e4CICQ), navigate to [myslice.syr.edu](https://myslice.syr.edu)
- Once there, click on the **Login** button. 

  ![A screenshot of the MySlice login page with the two buttons Login and Temporary NetID login. The Login option is highlighted with an arrow pointing to it.](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941796/Screenshot%202025-12-19%20142322.png?api=v2)
- When you get to the Microsoft Sign in screen **enter your NetID followed by "@syr.edu"**, for example, the NetID "otto44" would enter "otto44@syr.edu". Then click Next.

  ![microsoft login page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941796/myslice6v2.png?api=v2)

- If you encounter a screen that says "You don't have access to this", double check the email you used to login. The error usually means that a non-syr.edu email that does not have access to MySlice was used to login. Click **sign out and sign in with a different account** to login with the correct credentials.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941796/donthaveaccess.png?api=v2)

- Enter your NetID password
- If you need to change your password, you can do so at [netid.syr.edu](https://netid.syr.edu) using the **forgot your password** option. Check the [NetID Password FAQ](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/i4CICQ) for more information. 

  ![microsoft login password screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941796/test3.PNG?api=v2)
- If you have not configured multi-factor authentication yet, you will be prompted to configure before proceeding. Follow the on screen instructions. More instructions are available on the [Configuring Microsoft Multi-factor Authentication (MFA)](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/_IGICQ) page.

**Email access is not available to applicants or Project Advance (PA or SUPA) instructors or students.**

For **applicants**: an active email inbox is automatically generated within 24-48 hours **after the acceptance process is completed and the account status changes from applicant to active student**. Once generated, active students access their email address by going to [sumail.syr.edu](http://sumail.syr.edu) or in MySlice by clicking on **Student Resources > Syracuse Email**.
