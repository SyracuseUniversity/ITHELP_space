Many administrative departments at Syracuse University have additional information related to resources and support provided to users. Below you will find links to each department's websites and Answers space (if available).

**Not seeing the department you're looking for?** Users are encouraged to login with their NetID and password to view available content. Also note that not all departments on campus have information in Answers. Please search the [Syracuse University website](https://www.syracuse.edu/) for a complete list of University contacts.

**Looking for a School or College?**

School and college specific information and Answers spaces can be accessed through the [School and College page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/lIWICQ).

**Department IT Contact Information**

Contact methods and operating hours for IT personnel in department is available on the [departmental support contact page](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/).
