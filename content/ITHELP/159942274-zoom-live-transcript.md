Zoom offers integrated live transcription. The following steps assist in enabling this option within your [Syracuse University Zoom](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/ToGICQ) account.

---

## Before you Enable Live Transcripts

Before you enable live transcripts for your meeting participants, please note the following:

- If you have enabled the CC feature during your Zoom session/class, users must choose Save Transcript in the sidebar before the meeting ends if they want a copy.
- Speaker identification is included only in the real-time full transcript sidebar view and is not included in the downloaded transcript file.
- If you are a previous [Otter.ai](http://otter.ai/) user, stop Otter live stream (must be stopped for integrated Live Transcript to be available).

---

## Enabling Live Transcripts in Your Personal Profile Settings

Log in to [Zoom](https://zoom.syr.edu)

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Choose ‘**In Meeting (Advanced)**’
   1. Enable Closed Captioning
   2. Check the box next to ‘Enable live transcription service to show transcript on the side panel in-meeting.’
   3. Under **Save Captions** enable ‘Allow participants to save fully closed captions or transcripts (if desired, this is not required).

## Enabling Live Transcripts in Your Meeting

For each meeting that you would like live transcription made available:

1. Start the meeting
2. Click **CC/Live Transcript** button that appears on the Zoom toolbar
3. Click **Enable Auto-Transcription**

This should allow your participants to see a CC/Live Transcript button on their Zoom toolbar. Users will have the following options:

- Show/Hide subtitle
- View/close full transcript in the sidebar
- Change subtitle settings: font size

May Not Meet CART Requirements

Keep in mind, automated/machine-generated captioning DOES NOT replace CART as a disability-related accommodation. Continue to work with the [Center for Disability Resources](https://disabilityresources.syr.edu/) as needed.

Live Transcripts feature is not available in Zoom breakout rooms.

For additional information, see the [Closed Captioning and Live Transcription](https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/207279736-Closed-Captioning "https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/207279736-Closed-Captioning") page in the Zoom Help Center and Syracuse University's [Guidelines for CART and ASL Interpreting](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159942169/Guidelines+for+CART+and+ASL+Interpreting).
