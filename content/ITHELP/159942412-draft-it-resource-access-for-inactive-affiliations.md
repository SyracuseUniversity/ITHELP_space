This page provides information regarding IT resources available to the various Primary Affiliations that are held by former active users at Syracuse University. Below is information to assist in determining which inactive affiliation category a former active user falls into and which IT resources are available and for how long.

Users retaining resource access should note that many of the IT resources below require login with an active NetID and password. Users can manage their credentials on the [NetID self-service page](http://netid.syr.edu/).

## Draft IT Resource Access by Inactive Affiliation - Faculty/Staff Addressed, Students Not Addressed

**Note** - This section has been used to updated the [active inactive user page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/QYGICQ) (as of 10/19/21).

| Primary Affiliation | Affiliation Details | General IT Resource Access | Limiting Exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alumni and Student Inactive** | **Alumni** **affiliation is acquired** by former active students who have a graduated and completed a program of study at Syracuse University. The acquisition of this affiliation typically occurs **within 3 months of the completion of the program of study**.  **Student Inactive affiliation** is acquired by former active students that have discontinued their program of study and do not also have another completed or active program of study.  The acquisition of this affiliation typically occurs**within 30 days to 2 years of the last academic term dictating by the school or college**.  Former **SUPA students** also acquire student inactive affiliation **within 30 days of last academic term**. | Former students **retain access** to **most** **IT resources** for **12 months** following graduation.   Alumni **MySlice** access is retained for **24 months**. **Other former students** maintain access for **12 months**.   Former student **email, Blackboard, and Handshake**access is **maintained** through yearly password change requirements. Alumni that have never logged into Handshake can [create an account](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/SYJqCQ). | **Adobe** - Access to the Adobe Creative Cloud suite and its licensing **ends after graduation or leaving the University**. Cloud based assets and on-campus access to **Adobe Spark** for Higher Education, will expire **after 12 months**.  **Expressions** - Former students who had Expressions sites prior to August 1, 2021 can **access an archived copy** of their site information by logging into Expressions **thru July 31, 2022**.  **Printing** - While former students can log into lab machines for a period of time, their printing accounts as active users are no longer. A guest print account will be needed. Instructions to create the account can be found on the [Guest Printing](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/gIKICQ) page.  **Zoom** - Former students will **maintain** their Syracuse University Zoom account, however, the account will be moved to **a 'Basic' level account**. The Zoom Basic account will no longer have the benefits of a Zoom Edu Pro license. |
| **Faculty/Staff Inactive and Staff Retired** | **Staff Inactive** affiliation is acquired **the day after the job end date, as determined by Human Resources,**by former staff **who leave** the University **for any reason**, including resignation or retirement.  **Faculty Inactive** affiliation is acquired **the day after the job end date, as determined by Human Resources,** by former faculty **who leave** the University for any reason **but have not retired**. | Former **staff who have retired or left the University** and **faculty who have not retired** lose access to IT resources, including email, **the day after their last recorded day of work**. See the section below for exceptions. | **None**, former **staff who have retired or left the University** and **faculty who have not retired** are unable to authenticate to IT resources. |
| **Faculty Retired** | **Faculty Retired** affiliation is acquired by **former** **faculty who have retired,****including** **Emeritus** faculty. | Retired faculty **maintain access** to**many IT related resources** unless noted.  Faculty retired **email** access is **maintained** through yearly password change requirements.  **Note:** Access to some resources, such as **Adobe and Blackboard**, are **based on** the user having **previous access** as an active faculty member. | **Printing** - While retired faculty can log into lab machines, their printing accounts as active users are no longer. A guest print account will be needed. Instructions to create the account can be found on the [Guest Printing](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/gIKICQ) page.  **Zoom** - Retired faculty will **maintain** their Syracuse University Zoom account, however, the account will be moved to **a 'Basic' level account**. The Zoom Basic account will no longer have the benefits of a Zoom Edu Pro license. |

## Draft IT Resource Access by Inactive Affiliation - Both Students and Faculty/Staff Addressed

Below you will find descriptions and IT resources and access information for each inactive affiliation category. General IT Resource Access will describe the general access for system and notate any access beyond the normal timeframe. Limiting Exceptions will notate any access removed prior to the normal timeframe.

A list of IT resources is available on the [Services and Applications](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/s4KICQ) support page.

| Affiliation | Affiliation Details | General IT Resource Access | Limiting Exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alumni** | Alumni affiliation is **acquired within 30 days of last academic term** by former active students **who have graduated** or completed the minimum number of Syracuse University credits. | Alumni **retain access** to **most** **IT resources** for **12 months** following graduation.  Alumni **MySlice** access is retained for **24 months**.  Alumni **email, Blackboard, and Handshake**access is **maintained** through yearly password change requirements. Alumni that have never logged into Handshake can [create an account](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/SYJqCQ). | **Adobe** - Access to the Adobe Creative Cloud suite and its licensing **ends after graduation or leaving the University**. Cloud based assets and on-campus access to **Adobe Spark** for Higher Education, will expire **after 12 months**.  **Expressions** - Alumni who had Expressions sites prior to August 1, 2021 can **access an archived copy** of their site information by logging into Expressions **thru July 31, 2022**.  **Zoom** - Alumni will **maintain** their Syracuse University Zoom account, however, the account will be moved to **a 'Basic' level account**. The Zoom Basic account will no longer have the benefits of a Zoom Edu Pro license. |
| **Student Inactive** | Student Inactive affiliation is **acquired within 30 days of last academic term**by former students who are **no longer actively working towards an academic program** and who have **not completed the minimum** number of Syracuse University **credits**.  Former **SUPA students** also acquire student inactive affiliation **within 30 days of last academic term**. | Inactive students **lose access to IT resources**, including email, within **30 days of last academic term**by former students  who are **no longer actively working towards an academic program** and who have **not completed the minimum** number of Syracuse University **credits**. | **None**, former students **who are not alumni** are unable to authenticate to IT resources. |
| **Faculty/Staff Inactive and Staff Retired** | Staff Inactive affiliation is **acquired the day after last work date** by former staff who leave the University for any reason, including resignation or retirement.   Faculty Inactive affiliation is **acquired the day after last work date** by former faculty who leave the University for any reason but have not retired. | Former **staff who have retired or left the University** and **faculty who have not retired** lose access to IT resources, including email, **the day after their last recorded day of work**. See the section below for exceptions. | **None**, former **staff who have retired or left the University** and **faculty who have not retired** are unable to authenticate to IT resources. |
| **Faculty Retired** | Faculty Retired affiliation is acquired by former **faculty who have retired.** | Retired faculty **maintain access** to**most IT related resources** unless noted.  Faculty retired **email** access is **maintained** through yearly password change requirements.  **Note:** Access to some resources, such as **Adobe and Blackboard**, are **based on** the user having **previous access** as an active faculty member. | **Zoom** - Retired faculty will **maintain** their Syracuse University Zoom account, however, the account will be moved to **a 'Basic' level account**. The Zoom Basic account will no longer have the benefits of a Zoom Edu Pro license. |
