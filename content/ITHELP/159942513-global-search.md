Global Search is a useful way to find items by key terms, such as "grades" and "paycheck".

From the Student Home or Employee Home, enter search terms in the Search in All Content box in the header to locate the content you seek.  As you enter the terms, autofill search options are presented to help you identify your content more efficiently.  You may select one of the autofill options at any time.  If you do not see the content you seek in the autofill options, select the magnifying glass icon after you finish entering the search terms.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942513/Global%20Search%20from%20Homepage%20%5B20230216;%20943x34%5D.jpg?api=v2)

On most other pages, select the magnifying glass icon (![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942513/image2021-7-13_10-49-59.png?api=v2)) in the MySlice header to open the Search in All Content box.
