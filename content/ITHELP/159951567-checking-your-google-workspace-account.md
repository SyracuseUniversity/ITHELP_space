If you have any issues or concerns related to phishing in your **g.syr.edu** account through Syracuse University Google Workspace for Educations services, know that you can always do a check on your account through Google.

GMail Help through Google - <https://support.google.com/mail/answer/8253?hl=en>

- This URL will take you to Google's support page and give you additional information how you can identify, avoid, and report phishing emails that show up in your **g.syr.edu** account

Google is aware of large-scale attempts at phishing - and if you think you may have been affected, we recommend doing Google's Security Checkup:

- [https://myaccount.google.com/secureaccount](https://myaccount.google.com/secureaccount?utm_source=cnbc&utm_medium=tv&utm_campaign=cnbc-staying-safe-online)
- Please check that your Recovery phone number and Recovery email are correct
- Follow the additional steps, and verify that your account permissions are correct

We recommend going to [myaccount.google.com](http://myaccount.google.com) after you log in using your g.syr.edu account and checking to see what accounts you have authorized. Disable anything suspicious.

If you believe your account has been compromised - send an email to [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) with your name, back-up or recovery email, netID, and a description (screen-shots if you can).
