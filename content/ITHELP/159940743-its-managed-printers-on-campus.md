Students can use their print quota at the ITS-managed printers listed below.

**Note**

Other campus printers managed by Colleges or units (ex: the library) have been configured to use student print quotas – the pricing of the prints from these printers is determined by the College or department.

**Additional Information**

How to connect to ITS-managed wireless printers using Windows and macOS: [Windows and macOS](http://its.syr.edu/printing/wireless_printing_instructions.pdf)

Information on managing your print quota: [print quota](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/loGICQ)
