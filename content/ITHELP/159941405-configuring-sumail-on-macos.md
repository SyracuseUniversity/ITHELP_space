#### Getting SUMail set up in Outlook for Mac

1. Install Outlook

   To install Outlook, follow the instructions to download the Microsoft 365 app package on our [Microsoft 365 at Syracuse University page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/x/MICICQ).

   Faculty and/or Staff provided computers

   If you are using an SU-provided computer, Outlook is already installed. If it is **not** installed, contact your [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) IT support team to have it installed.
2. Launch Outlook

   Outlook can be launched from Finder by double-clicking the **Microsoft Outlook** icon in the Applications Folder, or by typing **Microsoft Outlook** in Spotlight at the top right of the screen, or by clicking on the Launchpad in the dock find the **Microsoft Outlook**icon and click it to launch it. 

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Outlook_In_Finder.png?api=v2)
3. Add your SUMail account

   When launching Outlook for the first time you will be prompted to set up an account. Enter your **full syr.edu email** (NetID@syr.edu) and password on the next screen.  

   Already have an account in Outlook?

   If you already have an account set up in Outlook you will need to add an additional account. On the menu bar click *Outlook > Preferences > Accounts and* hit the **+** button to add another account.

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Set_up_your_email.png?api=v2)

   4. **If prompted**, authenticate with two-factor.

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Authenticator_Screenshot.png?api=v2)

   5.   Once you have completed signing in you will see a confirmation message that your account has been added and you may begin using Outlook. 

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Account_Added.png?api=v2)

**Congratulations! Your SUMail is all set up!**

#### Getting SUMail setup for Apple Mail

*Steps shown below are using macOS 11.2 (Big Sur), your steps may vary visually depending on your operating system.*

1. Go to the Mail.app
   ![mail app](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2011.38.51%20AM.png?api=v2)
2. In Mail, choose **Mail** > **Add Account**

   ![add account highlighted in drop down menu](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2011.41.45%20AM.png?api=v2)
3. Select Microsoft Exchange

   ![select exchange account screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2011.50.04%20AM.png?api=v2)
4. Enter your full SUMail email address (NetID@[syr.edu](http://syr.edu))

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2011.58.12%20AM.png?api=v2)
5. Choose **Sign In**

   ![click sign in button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2011.58.20%20AM.png?api=v2)
6. Add your SUMail (NetID) password

   ![Enter password prompt](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2011.59.14%20AM.png?api=v2)
7. **If you are prompted** for Multifactor Authentication (MFA or 2FA), follow the on screen prompts to receive verification, otherwise skip this step

   ![approve the sign in request with the default method](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/7%20approve%202fa.png?api=v2)![approve MFA 2FA with an alternate method](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/8%20approve%202fa%202.png?api=v2)
8. Once you're logged in, choose what you would like to sync and then press **Done**.

   ![Click done to finish setting up account](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941405/Screen%20Shot%202021-02-21%20at%2012.00.07%20PM.png?api=v2)

   **Congratulations! Your SUMail is all set up!**
