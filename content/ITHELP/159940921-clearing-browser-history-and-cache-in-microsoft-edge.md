To clear browsing data on your computer, make sure **sync** is turned **off**. Items that are synced will be cleared across all synced devices. 

Here's how to clear your browsing data in Microsoft Edge:

1. Select **. . .**  > **Settings** 

   ![ellipses and then settings](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940921/01%20edge.png?api=v2)
2. Choose **Privacy, search, and services**
   ![privacy search and services circled](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940921/02%20privacy%20search.png?api=v2)
3. Under **Clear browsing data**, select **Choose what to clear**

   ![choose what to clear](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940921/03%20choose%20what%20to%20clear.png?api=v2)
4. Choose the **ALL TIME** range from the **Time range** drop-down menu

   ![choose from ALL TIME](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940921/04%20clear%20all%20time.png?api=v2)
5. Select **Clear now**

   ![clear now button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940921/05%20clear%20now.png?api=v2)
