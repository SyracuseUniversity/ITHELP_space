To link with Syracuse University identity a personal GitHub account is needed.  If you do not have a personal GitHub account, follow the instructions below:

- Navigate to [GitHub](https://github.com/) and select "Sign Up" near the top right-hand corner of the page.
- Enter your [syr.edu](http://syr.edu) email address.
- Create a password.

  - Best practice is to have a **different**password than your Syracuse University NetID.
- Create a username.
- Solve the captcha and create your account.
- After creating your account, multifactor will need to be enabled - navigate to the [GitHub Passkey Setup](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/IoOICQ) and follow the instructions for MFA.
