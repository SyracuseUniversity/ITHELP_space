[**Syracuse University students have access to special pricing on select laptops and devices through the University’s partner, SHI.**](https://its.syr.edu/studentdiscount/)

Below are recommendations are for most incoming students and their families. All configurations include a minimum of 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage — the baseline for Microsoft 365, university software, and modern coursework. For information on how to purchase any of these, or other items with special pricing, please visit:
 <https://its.syr.edu/studentdiscount/>

**Your school or college may have specific requirements.** Students in Architecture, Engineering, Computer Science, Visual and Performing Arts, and some other programs need hardware that supports particular software. Before purchasing, check [Recommendations for Student Computers](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941128/Recommendations+for+Student+Computers) to see if your school or college has additional guidance.

**Microsoft 365 is free for all active Syracuse University students** — install it on up to five computers and five mobile devices. See the [Office 365 at Syracuse University](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/MICICQ) page for details.

## Better: For Most Students

These configurations handle general coursework, writing, research, web-based tools, video calls, and light creative work comfortably.

### Microsoft Surface Laptop 13.8-inch (ARM)

**Snapdragon X Plus (10-core) | 16GB RAM | 512GB SSD**

A lightweight Windows laptop with up to 20 hours of battery life and a 120Hz HDR touchscreen. A Copilot+ PC with a dedicated NPU that handles AI tasks locally — no cloud required — making it a strong option for students interested in AI tools and local language model work. Windows 11 handles most mainstream x86 software through a built-in compatibility layer.

**Extended Warranty:** We strongly recommend adding [Microsoft Complete](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/b/microsoft-complete) protection at the time of purchase. Microsoft Complete covers mechanical breakdown and accidental damage (drops, spills, and screen breakage) and includes prepaid return shipping. Choose the **3-year or 4-year plan** for the best value over a typical college career. Coverage must be added at or near the time of purchase and cannot be added later.

### Apple MacBook Air 13-inch (M5)

**16GB Unified Memory | 512GB SSD**

A fanless, completely silent laptop weighing 2.7 pounds with up to 18 hours of battery life. Runs all mainstream Mac software natively, including Microsoft 365 and Adobe Creative Cloud. Available in Sky Blue, Midnight, Starlight, and Silver.

**Extended Warranty:** We strongly recommend adding [AppleCare+](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/SGFM2LL/A/applecare-for-macbook-air) at the time of purchase. AppleCare+ extends coverage to three years and includes unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection (subject to a service fee), priority access to Apple support, and battery service coverage. It can be added within 60 days of purchase but is most convenient to add at the time of purchase.

## Best: For Students with More Demanding Workloads

Recommended for students doing video editing, design, software development, data analysis, heavy multitasking, or AI and local language model work.

### Microsoft Surface Laptop 13.8-inch (ARM)

**Snapdragon X Elite (12-core) | 32GB RAM | 1TB SSD**

The 12-core Elite processor and 32GB of RAM provide meaningful headroom for demanding creative and technical workloads. The dedicated NPU paired with 32GB makes this the strongest Surface option for students running local AI models on-device. Available in Black only at this configuration.

**Extended Warranty:** We strongly recommend adding [Microsoft Complete](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/b/microsoft-complete) protection at the time of purchase. Choose the **3-year or 4-year plan** for the best value over a typical college career. Coverage must be added at or near the time of purchase and cannot be added later.

### Apple MacBook Air 13-inch (M5)

**24GB Unified Memory | 1TB SSD**

Apple's unified memory architecture makes 24GB highly efficient across CPU, GPU, and AI tasks. A good fit for students doing video editing, design, software development, or sustained multitasking. All four colors are available at this configuration.

**Extended Warranty:** We strongly recommend adding [AppleCare+](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/SGFM2LL/A/applecare-for-macbook-air) at the time of purchase. AppleCare+ extends coverage to three years and includes unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection (subject to a service fee), priority access to Apple support, and battery service coverage. It can be added within 60 days of purchase but is most convenient to add at the time of purchase.

## If Your Program Requires Windows x86 Software

Some programs require specialized software — such as MATLAB, Ansys, AutoCAD, or SolidWorks — that does not run reliably on ARM-based computers or macOS. If your school or college has specific hardware requirements, check [Recommendations for Student Computers](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941128/Recommendations+for+Student+Computers) before purchasing.

If you need a Windows laptop with full native x86 compatibility, the Surface Laptop for Business with Intel is the recommended option.

### Microsoft Surface Laptop for Business 13.8-inch (Intel)

**Better: Intel Core Ultra 5 (Series 2) | 16GB RAM | 512GB SSD**

Available in Black and Platinum. A 15-inch model with similar specs is also available.

**Best: Intel Core Ultra 7 (Series 2) | 32GB RAM | 1TB SSD**

Available in Black and Platinum. A 15-inch model with the same specs is also available.

**Extended Warranty:** We strongly recommend adding [Microsoft Complete](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/store/b/microsoft-complete) protection at the time of purchase. Choose the **3-year or 4-year plan** for the best value over a typical college career. Coverage must be added at or near the time of purchase and cannot be added later.
