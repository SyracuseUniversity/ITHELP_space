Students can create an account for their proxy so they can access the student’s information within MySlice and pay bills.

Log into MySlice and select the “Share My Access” link on the “Student Home” page.

![Share My Access tile on the Student home page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/458982059/image-20250901-192759.png?api=v2)

A listing of existing proxy accounts appears. These can be edited or deleted. The page also has a button to add a new proxy account.

![List of proxy accounts](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/458982059/Proxy%20List.png?api=v2)

If you add a new proxy, you’ll be prompted to enter name and email address information. You also select what access this account should have to your academic and personal information.

![Enter name and email address for the proxy and select what access should be granted](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/458982059/Add%20a%20proxy.png?api=v2)

After the account information is saved, the proxy receives two emails:

1. An email from SU informing the proxy that the account was created and they will be receiving an email from Microsoft to complete setup of the account.
2. An invitation email from Microsoft. For more information on accepting the email and completing setup of the account, see [Accepting the Microsoft Invite for Proxy Access to MySlice](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/448659767/Accepting+the+Microsoft+Invite+for+Proxy+Access+to+MySlice)

Students can create **up to three (3) proxy accounts** using different email addresses for each account. Each proxy account can be configured with different levels of access.
