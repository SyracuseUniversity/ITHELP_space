Handshake is the career platform used by Syracuse University students, staff, and faculty. Career staff that utilize the calendar inside of Handshake can sync the calendar with their SUMail account using the steps below. More details and instructions are also available on the [Welcome to Handshake page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/BYBqCQ).

---

## Connecting Handshake to SUMail

1. Visit [https://syr.joinhandshake.com](https://syr.joinhandshake.com/) and click on **Syracuse University Login**

   ![SU login box for Handshake](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/Screen%20Shot%202021-03-18%20at%208.55.39%20AM.png?api=v2)
2. At the NetID Login box, enter your NetID and Password

   ![Shibboleth login for NetID and Password](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/Screen%20Shot%202021-03-18%20at%208.55.54%20AM.png?api=v2)
3. Once in Handshake, click on **your name** in the upper right hand corner, then click **User Settings**

   ![First click your name, then User Settings](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/Screen%20Shot%202021-03-18%20at%208.57.03%20AM.png?api=v2)
4. Click on **Calendar Sync** on the left side navigation menu

   ![calendar sync page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/02%20-%20calendar%20sync.PNG?api=v2)
5. Click on **Authorize****Calendar Sync**

   ![authorize calendar sync](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/03.5-authorizecalendarsync.png?api=v2)
6. You will be shown a list of calendar providers. Choose **Office 365** from the list

   ![choose outlook 365 from calendar list](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/03%20-%20authorize%20to%20provider.PNG?api=v2)
7. Check the box agreeing to the Cronofy Terms of Service, then click **Link Office 365 Account

   ![link calendar](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/04%20-%20authorize%20and%20link.PNG?api=v2)**
8. You will be prompted to **Pick an account**. Choose your syr.edu email from the list. If you are not presented with your syr.edu account, simply type in your full email address (netid@syr.edu) then click next

   ![pick and account pop up](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/05%20-%20pick%20an%20account.PNG?api=v2)
9. When prompted for permission, click **Accept**. After a few moments, you will return to the Handshake Calendar Sync page

   ![permissions requested](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/06%20-%20grant%20permissions.PNG?api=v2)
10. Finally, in the **Choose a Calendar** drop down menu, make sure your calendar is selected. 

    ![calendar drop down](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942493/08%20-%20choose%20a%20calendar.jpg?api=v2)
11. Congratulations! Your calendar is now synced.
