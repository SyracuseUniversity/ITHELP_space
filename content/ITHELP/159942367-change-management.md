Change Management at Syracuse University is intended to ensure all changes to university information technology resources are well planned, tested, coordinated, communicated, and successful in a timely fashion and with the least possible negative impact on productivity and users.  

Change Management includes a formal, centralized process of efficient and prompt authorization, scheduling, communication, and control to promptly accommodate all changes in order to ensure that the IT infrastructure stays aligned to business and service requirements as well as to minimize the risk of change-related incidents or degradations of service quality.

Change Management is led by change managers and consists of specified ITS personnel. The team provides appropriate ITS-wide representation and consistent change direction and communications. IT community members can find more details on the [Change Management at Syracuse University](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/C4BBCQ) page (access restricted).
