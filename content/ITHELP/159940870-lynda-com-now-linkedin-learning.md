Looking for Lynda.com? Syracuse University has upgraded Lynda.com to LinkedIn Learning  to help active faculty, staff, and students explore thousands of business, tech, and creative courses to get the skills you need. 

Full LinkedIn Learning details can be found on the [LinkedIn Learning at Syracuse University home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/SIOICQ). 

Active users can log in by visiting [linkedinlearning.syr.edu](https://linkedinlearning.syr.edu/).
