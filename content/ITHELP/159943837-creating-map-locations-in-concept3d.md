Locations in Concept3D are used to pinpoint specific buildings and places on the map by using a marker icon, and to provide related information including descriptions,  pictures, and videos. They are found within the map sidebar under the "Locations" section and are typically organized together with related locations within a category or sub-category.

Vendor documentation: <https://help.concept3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005790267-Creating-A-New-Location>

## Creating a Map Location

1. Log into the CMS: [Accessing the CMS for Concept3D](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159948603/Accessing+the+CMS+for+Concept3D)
2. Select **NEW LOCATION**
   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159943837/Screen%20Shot%202020-02-17%20at%2010.42.13%20AM.png?api=v2)
3. Fill out the fields as desired. See the [Location Options in Concept3D](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159944020/Location+Options+in+Concept3D) Answers page for more information on the available options.
4. Select **Save** in the top-left of the sidebar.
