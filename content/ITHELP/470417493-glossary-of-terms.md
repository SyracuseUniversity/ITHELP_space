This glossary explains common terms you may see in OrangeAccess. Use it as a quick reference when reading documentation or using the system.

---

**Access Profile**
A group of entitlements from a single source, often used for common job needs within one application.

**Access Request**
The process to request or remove access. May involve approvals from a manager or application/data owner.

**Access Requests to Review**
Tile on the home page where approvers see requests that require their review.

**Aggregation**
Collecting account data from connected systems (e.g., AD, PeopleSoft).

**Application**
A system or service available in OrangeAccess. Once selected, you can view a list of available access items (entitlements and access profiles).

**Approval Workflow**
The path a request takes through manager and application/data owner reviews before access is granted.

**Certification**
A periodic review of access by managers or system owners to confirm or revoke access.

**Certification Campaign**
A scheduled set of certifications where reviewers must act on all assigned access items for their users.

**Correlation**
Matching accounts to identities during aggregation.

**Delegation**
Forwarding or reassigning a request to another approver.

**Entitlement**
A specific permission or access right (for example, the ability to run a report).

**Expiration Date**
Optional setting on a request that ends access on a specific date.

**Governance Group**
A group of application or data owners who share responsibility for approving requests.

**Identity**
A person or service account with access to systems.

**Identity Lifecycle**
Stages of an identity’s status at the University: Joiner, Mover, Leaver. These events trigger access updates.

**Manager**
The requester’s supervisor. Usually the first approver in the workflow.

**Notification Email**
Automated message sent when a request is submitted, approved, denied, or revoked.

**Owner**
The person or group responsible for approving access to a specific application or data set.

**Pending Requests**
Tile on the home page where you can track the status of your submitted requests.

**Request Center**
Central page in OrangeAccess where you can request access for yourself, your team, or others.

**Revocation**
The removal of existing access, either by request or during a certification.

**Role**
A bundle of access profiles tied to a job function or responsibility, often spanning multiple applications.

**Tile**
The clickable panels on the OrangeAccess home page that link to features (for example, Pending Requests, Request Center).

**Work Reassignment**
Feature that allows an approver to delegate request reviews to another person temporarily.
