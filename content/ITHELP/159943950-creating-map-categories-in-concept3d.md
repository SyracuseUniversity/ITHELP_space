Categories in Concept3D are containers for sub-categories and locations. They are used to organize the map sidebar and to group related locations together within a category. Sub-categories can be used for further division and organization of locations. Categories are found within the map sidebar under the "Locations" section.

Vendor Documentation: <https://help.concept3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023798134-Creating-A-New-Category->

## Creating a Map Category

1. Log into the CMS: [Accessing the CMS for Concept3D](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159948603/Accessing+the+CMS+for+Concept3D)
2. Select **NEW CATEGORY**
   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159943950/Screen%20Shot%202020-02-14%20at%2011.02.27%20AM.png?api=v2)
3. Fill out the fields as desired. See the [Category Options in Concept3D](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159945163/Category+Options+in+Concept3D) Answers page for more information on the available options.
4. Select **Save** in the top-left of the sidebar.
