To join an existing meeting, you'll need the email invite or the meeting ID and the passcode.

## Joining a Meeting (Desktop App)

1. Open the [Zoom.us](http://Zoom.us) desktop app and select the **Join** button.

   ![Zoom main window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159951064/Screen%20Shot%202020-09-02%20at%202.48.20%20PM.png?api=v2)
2. Enter your [meeting ID](https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/201362373-What-is-a-Meeting-ID-) provided by the host/organizer.
3. Click **Join**. You will be asked to open the Zoom client to join the meeting.

## Joining a Meeting (Web Browser)

1. Open any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
2. If you are a standard Zoom user, use the following page: [Zoom at Syracuse University](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940942/Zoom+at+Syracuse+University) and click the **Login to SU Zoom Account** button.

   **HIPAA Compliant** users, use this page: [HIPAA Compliant Zoom at Syracuse University](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159943131/HIPAA+Compliant+Zoom+at+Syracuse+University) and click the **Login to HIPAA Zoom Account** button.

   ![zoom video conferencing join window screenshot](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159951064/Screen%20Shot%202020-04-09%20at%2010.38.45%20AM.png?api=v2)
3. Enter your [meeting ID](https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/201362373-What-is-a-Meeting-ID-) provided by the host/organizer.

![join a meeting ID window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159951064/join-a-meeting-web.png?api=v2)

4.  Click **Join**. You will be asked to open the Zoom client to join the meeting.
