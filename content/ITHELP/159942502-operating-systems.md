There are several commonly used Operating Systems that are used on campus that we can provide support with. Please choose from the list below for the one that you need help with to see if the contents within will assist you. If they do not, please reach out to our Service Center and we will assist to our best ability. In some cases you may need more direct support from the manufacturer or other servicing options based on the device. If you require further assistance our information can be found at the bottom of the page.

- [Apple](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/display/ITHELP/Apple)
- [Microsoft Windows](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/display/ITHELP/Microsoft+Windows)
- [Check OS Installation Dates](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/display/ITHELP/Check+OS+Installation+Dates)

## Getting Help

For support of the information above, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc) by calling at 315.443.2677, by emailing [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu), or by visiting in person at the ITS Service Center. Further information regarding hours and location can be found on the [ITS Service Center](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/) page.
