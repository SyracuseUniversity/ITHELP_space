## OneDrive at Syracuse University

---

OneDrive is the default storage location for personal files at SU.  Saving to OneDrive on an SU managed machine is as simple as saving to Documents or Desktop. OneDrive lets you store and protect your files, share them with others, and access them from anywhere on all your devices.

Additionally, it used to be known as "OneDrive for Business," so you may still see it called that in places throughout Answers.

### Accessing OneDrive on the Web

---

Sign into [office.syr.edu](https://office.syr.edu) and click on the App Launcher menu button (![A screencap of a bento button made up of nine dots in a 3x3 grid. Commonly used to denote additional menu options](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRFMB--JDhc5ckdYqnELTBK4ArBIGUSPUOKZNJAOPujHKaryz_CxYUpUEKOG43YXV9XJp8&usqp=CAU)) at the top left of the page and choose OneDrive from the list of applications.

### Specific to Syracuse University OneDrive

---

#### Sharing

SU requires that sharing invitations be sent to a specific email address. External recipients who are not using a @syr.edu email address will be added as a guest in the university's directory and must verify their identity before accessing shared files.

#### Storage

OneDrive storage quotas vary by license type assigned to the user.  Staff and Faculty can request more storage from their IT support.
