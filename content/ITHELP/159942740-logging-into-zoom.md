Students, Faculty, Staff, and Syracuse University affiliates can log into the Syracuse University Zoom system using Single Sign-On (SSO) and your Syracuse University credentials. When signing into Zoom, you must use the **Sign In with SSO (single sign-on)**option and enter **syracuseuniversity** for the company domain.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/ZoomSignInScreen123.png?api=v2)

Only Single Sign-On (SSO) is permitted and the following **Sign In** methods will no longer be permitted:

- Google
- Apple
- Facebook

## Logging into Zoom (Desktop App)

1. Open Zoom desktop client and click **Sign In**.

   ![Zoom opening screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/Screen%20Shot%202020-09-02%20at%202.41.27%20PM.png?api=v2)
2. Choose the **Sign In with SSO** button.

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/ZoomSignInScreen123.png?api=v2)
3. If prompted, enter **syracuseuniversity** for the company domain. This is not case sensitive but is all one word with no spaces. Then click continue. 
   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/Screen%20Shot%202020-09-02%20at%202.44.23%20PM.png?api=v2)
4. A browser window will open and ask for your NetID and password. Enter both and click **Log In**.

   ![shibboleth window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/Screen%20Shot%202020-09-02%20at%202.45.55%20PM.png?api=v2)
5. Your browser will give a pop-up asking if it can open in [Zoom.us](http://Zoom.us). Choose **Allow**.

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/Screen%20Shot%202020-09-02%20at%202.47.05%20PM.png?api=v2)
6. The Zoom desktop app will now open and you can use Zoom normally as an SU licensed user.

   ![Zoom main window](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942740/Screen%20Shot%202020-09-02%20at%202.48.20%20PM.png?api=v2)

## Logging into Zoom (Web Browser)

You can use the following web links:

1. Main Syracuse University Zoom system - <https://syracuseuniversity.zoom.us/>
2. HIPAA/PHI Compliant Syracuse University Zoom system - <https://phi-syracuseuniversity.zoom.us/>
