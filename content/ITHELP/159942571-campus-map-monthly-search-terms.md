## Overview

---

This page contains a report of the top 50 ![(star)](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/s/420756305/6452/64520b994918eab4c38fbbf6489e823b4a83bea4/_/images/icons/emoticons/star_yellow.png) search terms for the last month. Comes out the first Monday the following month.

- **Search Term** The term that was searched.
- **Total Events ![(star)](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/s/420756305/6452/64520b994918eab4c38fbbf6489e823b4a83bea4/_/images/icons/emoticons/star_yellow.png)** The total number of searches on that term. Includes counts where terms were searched multiple times in one session.
- **Unique Events** Number of unique sessions that the term was searched.
