## Add a Shared Mailbox to Outlook for Windows 10

1. Launch Outlook
2. Click on **File**

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202021-03-03%20092455.png?api=v2)
3. Click on **+ Add Account**

   ![6329c8a2-eac3-4ffd-8a26-ebb22bf8e458.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/6329c8a2-eac3-4ffd-8a26-ebb22bf8e458.png?api=v2)
4. Enter the **email address for the shared mailbox** and click **Connect**

   ![shared mailbox.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/shared%20mailbox.png?api=v2)
5. If prompted, choose **Office 365** 

   ![a7be4225-6d31-4b81-8590-c1d66866eca6.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/a7be4225-6d31-4b81-8590-c1d66866eca6.png?api=v2)
6. Click on **Sign in with another account**

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202021-03-03%20093750.png?api=v2)
7. Replace the shared mailbox email address with **your full email address** (NetID@syr.edu) then click **Sign in**

   ![488cfa9a-54f1-472e-a57a-e3b8a5f082bb.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/488cfa9a-54f1-472e-a57a-e3b8a5f082bb.png?api=v2)
8. Enter in the password for **your email account**

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202021-03-03%20093850.png?api=v2)
9. When prompted, authenticate with your two-factor authentication method (2FA)

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202021-03-03%20093912.png?api=v2)
10. It may take a moment to link the shared mailbox then you will see a prompt that states "Account successfully added." Click **Done** and **restart Outlook**

    ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202021-03-03%20093942.png?api=v2)
11. After you restart Outlook, you will see the shared mailbox added and it is now ready to be used.

    ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202021-03-03%20094148.png?api=v2)

## Add a Shared Mailbox to Outlook for macOS

1. Launch Outlook
2. Click on **File**> **Open**> **Shared Mailbox...**

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/01%20walkthru%20to%20shared%20mailbox.png?api=v2)
3. A pop-up window will appear. Search for the account you have access to share in the search bar. You can search by name or by address. Click the blue **Add** button to add the account.  

   ![e52d3edd-bad2-4307-949d-66f5647f881e.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/e52d3edd-bad2-4307-949d-66f5647f881e.png?api=v2)
4. As long as you have permission to the account, it will added in the left sidebar of Outlook for Mac. You may now use the account.

   ![b487196a-5276-4576-afe5-b5463e3f2cc3.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/b487196a-5276-4576-afe5-b5463e3f2cc3.png?api=v2)

## Add a Shared Mailbox to Outlook for mobile (iOS and Android)

1. Launch Outlook
2. Click on **your image/your initials** in the upper left corner

   ![icon for outlook](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/1%20tap%20icon.png?api=v2)
3. Click on the (**+**) **Envelope** icon

   ![c7fea95e-d5a2-4359-99b2-2c872114173b.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/c7fea95e-d5a2-4359-99b2-2c872114173b.png?api=v2)
4. Click on **Add Shared Mailbox**

   ![0adf14c7-595e-41c6-bc15-d7db815c8335.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/0adf14c7-595e-41c6-bc15-d7db815c8335.png?api=v2)
5. Enter the email address of the **shared account**

   ![enter shared email address](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/4%20enter%20email%20account.png?api=v2)
6. The **shared account will now appear in your side bar** and is able to be accessed

   ![added account shows as an icon on the left bar](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/5%20all%20done.png?api=v2)

## Connect to a Shared Mailbox on Outlook on the Web (OWA)

1. Sign in to your account at [sumail.syr.edu](https://sumail.syr.edu)
2. Click on **your image/your initials** in the upper right corner then click on **Open another mailbox**

   ![screenshot of corner of SUMail.syr.edu](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screen%20Shot%202021-03-08%20at%205.37.19%20PM.png?api=v2)
3. Enter the **email address of the account you have access to** and click open

   ![open another mailbox](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screen%20Shot%202021-03-08%20at%205.39.41%20PM.png?api=v2)

## Add a Shared Mailbox to New Outlook

1. Launch New Outlook

2. Right-click on your email address. This should appear above your inbox’s navigation pane on the left side of the screen.

3. Right-clicking on your email address will open a context menu. Within this context menu, select “*Add shared folder or mailbox”*

![Screenshot 2026-01-07 110139.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202026-01-07%20110139.png?api=v2)

4. Enter your desired address within the “*Add a shared email account”* window then hit “Continue”.

   ![Screenshot 2026-01-07 110245.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/Screenshot%202026-01-07%20110245.png?api=v2)

5. You may be prompted to take additional steps depending on your email provider. Most involve logging into the other inbox to approve the request.

![newoutlook3.jpg](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940958/newoutlook3.jpg?api=v2)

## Getting Help

If you have trouble connecting after following the above instructions or need additional support, faculty, staff, and students should contact the [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel that **manages the shared mailbox you are trying to connect to**.
