## About the App

Syracuse University has integrated bus and trolley information into the OrangeNow app and displays the following:

- Display Centro routes
- Display Syracuse University trolley routes
- Display all current vehicles on each of the routes
- Predicted arrival times for the current vehicles at each stop
- Available stops for each route
- Graphical pattern of each route
- Any service bulletins/notices for the routes around campus

The SU Bus app allows you to check the current location of available buses as well as the estimated arrival times for stops along the following routes:

- Waverly Ave (SU43)
- Manley (SU44)
- Slocum Heights (SU244)
- Nob Hill (SU245)
- South Campus (SU344)
- Connective Corridor (SU443)
- Small Road (SU444)
- East Campus (SU243)
- James-Westcott (SU145)
- Westcott - SU (SY30)
- Drumlins - Nob Hill (SY40)
- SU Trolley - Orange Loop
- SU Trolley - Blue Loop

More information on Centro buses and schedules, please use the following link: [Higher Education](https://www.centro.org/routes-schedules/higher-education/#top)

## Instructions

Locate the ***bus*** or ***trolley*** icon on the main screen of the SU Mobile app

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/trolleyBK.png?api=v2)![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/trolley.png?api=v2)

You can also use this app on a desktop/laptop computer: <https://subus.syr.edu/>

The **map view** shows all stops for Centro's SU and trolley routes. **If you have location services enabled on your device, the map will center on your position.**

## Map View (default)

#### To get arrival times for a given stop:

1. Find any bus stop, identified as an orange and blue icon.

   ![blue dot with an orange exterior ring](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/subus_stopIcon.jpeg?api=v2)

     You can scroll and zoom the map to find the stop you are looking for.

   ![Map with stops and buses](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-8-48.png?api=v2)
2. Click on the bus stop icon and information about that stop will be displayed. The information can be expanded by clicking on the name of the stop in the balloon or at the bottom of the screen.

   ![Stop selected on map with balloon reading college place](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-9-21.png?api=v2)

#### To get information about a given bus:

1. Click on the vehicle icon and information about that bus will be displayed. The bus route will also be displayed on the map.

   ![bus selected on map with balloon reading SU344 - 344 South Campus](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-10-46.png?api=v2)

### Search / Form View

To search for a bus number, street name, or part of a name, click on the magnifying glass icon.

![search button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-11-22.png?api=v2)

1. Search using the text box.

   ![search window open](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-11-45.png?api=v2)
2. Your search results will display below. Selecting the ***Show Stop On Map*** or the ***Show Bus On Map*** button will bring you back to the map and display the data.

   ![manley searched with results below](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-12-11.png?api=v2)

### Service Bulletin View

![bulletins button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-12-47.png?api=v2)

Selecting the Exclamation mark icon,  will switch you to the service bulletin screen. This will display any data Centro has added to the routes.

![informational service bulletins shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941114/image2019-2-19_8-12-31.png?api=v2)
