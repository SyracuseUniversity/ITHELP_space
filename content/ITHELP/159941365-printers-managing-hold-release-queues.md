# Managing Hold/Release Queues

In some cases, the hold/release queue of a printer may be restricting other users from printing, or simply need to be managed manually. The following steps are available to administrators in the Hold Release role in the Printers tab.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/printersloc.PNG?api=v2)

### 1 - Locate the Hold Release Queue

Once in the Printers tab in the admin interface, click on Jobs Pending Release.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/printerjobsqueue.png?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941365/Printers+-+Managing+Hold+Release+Queues#Printers-ManagingHold/ReleaseQueues-topofpa)

### 2 - Filter Jobs Pending Release List

By default, all pending print jobs will be visible. Clicking on "**Filter on**" (or Filter off if there instead) to reveal additional filtering options. After selecting the desired parameters be sure to click "**Apply**" to apply the filter.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/jobfilteroptions.PNG?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941365/Printers+-+Managing+Hold+Release+Queues#Printers-ManagingHold/ReleaseQueues-topofpa)

### 3 - Review the Print Jobs Pending Release

The list of jobs pending release will break down each pending print job. Information will include the submission date/time, intended printer, what user printed it, page count, and cost.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/releaseq.PNG?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941365/Printers+-+Managing+Hold+Release+Queues#Printers-ManagingHold/ReleaseQueues-topofpa)

### 4 - Performing Actions

Pay special attention to the Actions column. Here options will include the ability to manually print, cancel or override a pending print job.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/releaseqa.PNG?api=v2)

#### **Print**

Click Print to manually print the job at the selected printer notated in the Printer column.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/releasep.png?api=v2)

The job will be removed from the list after printing.

#### **Cancel**

Click Cancel to manually cancel the job. Note that the client will not be charged.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/releasec.png?api=v2)

The job will be removed from the list after cancelling.

#### **Override**

Click Override to change the amount the client will be charged for the print job.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/releaseo.png?api=v2)

Clicking Override will reveal cost options. Select to either charge nothing, or input a manual amount. When finished, click "**Print**" to complete the override. The job will be removed from the list after printing.
![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941365/releaseov.PNG?api=v2)

Comments Required

A detailed comment is required for all overrides without exception.
