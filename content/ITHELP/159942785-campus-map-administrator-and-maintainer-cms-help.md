## Overview

---

The documentation contained within this section is for users who create, maintain, and administer the categories, locations, tours, and related content from the Administrator Content Management System (CMS) of the Syracuse University interactive campus map (aka Concept3D application). If you are seeking general help on using the map interface, please see the <https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941766> Answers page.
