**Learning Remotely?**

If you are a distance learner or simply unable to visit campus, be sure to check out the [Online Success Toolkit](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/AoCsCQ) developed in partnership with the Office of Online Student Success.

**Teaching or Working Remotely?**

Check out our [Working & Teaching Remotely page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/PIWICQ) if you need to work remotely, traveling, or simply unable to visit campus due to prolonged campus or building closures.

### **Accessible Toolkit**

[![Accessibility logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941762/access.png?api=v2)](https://itsaccessibility.syr.edu/)

Information and Communication Technology accessibility at SU means ensuring that all technology is usable by the greatest number of students, faculty and staff possible, including those with disabilities, so that everyone is able to participate to the maximum extent that they can. Additional information including getting assistance, workshops, and policies can be found on the [ITS Accessibility website](https://itsaccessibility.syr.edu/). 

[Accessible Toolkit Home Page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/j4CICQ)

![blackboard ultra logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941762/blackboardu.png?api=v2)

[Blackboard](http://blackboard.syracuse.edu) learning management software makes class content available to students via the Internet. Blackboard provides students and instructors with interactive web 2.0 technologies such as blogs and wikis.

Assistance with Blackboard can be found on the [Blackboard self-help page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/JYMzCQ).

[Blackboard Home Page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/JYMzCQ)

### **Online Learning Services**

[![Logo of a computer with a graduation cap on it](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941762/online-learning.png?api=v2)](https://ols.syr.edu/)

Online Learning Services is a unit within Information Technology Services at Syracuse University. Their primary mission is to provide leadership and support in the appropriate use of technology for teaching, learning, and research. 

Full details including faculty forms, educational tips, and contact information, can be found on the [Online Learning Services website](https://ols.syr.edu/)

### **Course Feedback**

[![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941762/feedback.png?api=v2)](https://effectiveness.syr.edu/course-feedback/)

Gathering and providing course feedback from students at the end of each course is an important activity that the Institutional Effectiveness and Assessment (IEA) facilitates.

Please visit IEA’s [Course Feedback](https://effectiveness.syr.edu/course-feedback/) page for more information on the course feedback process, tool, and FAQs.
