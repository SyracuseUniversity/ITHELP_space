**The first step to creating a video out of your Power Point Presentation is to**

- Select the Slide Show Tab
- Select Record Slide
- Select Record from Beginning

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940729/1.png?api=v2)

**After selecting Record Slide Show check both boxes and then select Start Recording**

**To stop recording you can either click stop and then exit or just press the “x” in the top right corner and it will save to your video in the presentation. The file should be saved in WMV format.**

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940729/3.png?api=v2)

**Lastly when the file is saved there will be a power point slide show version and a WMV version.  The WMV version is what will be uploaded to ensemble.**

![File will be both powerpoint and video file](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940729/Saved_files.PNG?api=v2)
