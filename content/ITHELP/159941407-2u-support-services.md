## Zoom

Please note that 2U has not adopted the Syracuse University implementation of Zoom. When hosting or participating in classes, you should continue to access Zoom from the 2U learning management system (LMS) using the original login provided by 2U. 2SU faculty and students that wish to log into the [zoom.us](https://zoom.us/) portal should do so with your 2U username and password, provided by 2U and is formatted **firstinitiallastname+XXXX**@[onlinebusiness.syr.edu.prod.zoom.2u.com](http://onlinebusiness.syr.edu.prod.zoom.2u.com/) or **firstinitiallastname+XXX**@[syr.edu.prod.zoom.2u.com](http://syr.edu.prod.zoom.2u.com/).

**Students** needing support with your 2U Zoom account, please contact your 2U school or college support contact listed above.

**Faculty** experiencing **any** issues using your 2U Zoom account, please contact 2U Tech Support via live chat through the digital platform, via phone at 877-552-0628, or by email at [facultysupport@2u.com](mailto:facultysupport@2u.com).
