## About Microsoft Planner at Syracuse University

#### What does Planner provide?

Microsoft Planner makes it easy for your team to create new plans, organize and assign tasks, share files, chat about what you’re working on, and get updates on progress.

- Organize work visually
- Collaborate around tasks
- Assign tasks and update progress

#### What to know about Planner at Syracuse.

- Tasks can only be assigned to users that appear in the search provided by Planner.
