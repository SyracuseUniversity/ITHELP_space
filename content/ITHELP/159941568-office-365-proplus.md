Office 365 ProPlus installations are offered to all **active** Students, Faculty, and Staff members through Syracuse University's licensing agreement with Microsoft.

Through the program, current SU & ESF students (undergraduate, graduate, part-time, and non-credit) and SU staff and faculty (not ESF Staff or Faculty) can download and install Office desktop applications on up to five computers (PC, Mac) and five mobile devices (iPhone, iPad, Android).

Project Advance students are **not** eligible for Office 365 ProPlus.

ESF students may need to change their password if they have problems with downloading the app, or if there is a "Read Only" mode message.

### Staying Active with Office 365 ProPlus

---

Office applications installed through our licensing program remain activated as long as a student or staff/faculty remains eligible.  When you graduate or leave the university, the subscription will expire. All users must connect any device with Office 365 ProPlus to the Internet at least once every 30 days for an automatic license check.

### Power BI

---

[Power BI](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/en-us/features/) is included with the Office 365 ProPlus subscription. Power BI is a suite of business analytics tools to analyze data and share insights. Users can monitor the health of their business using a live dashboard, create rich interactive reports with [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop) and access data on the go with native [Power BI Mobile](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/mobile) apps.

### Sign In Support

If you are unable to sign in using your SU email and password simply change your password on the [NetID self-service page](http://netid.syr.edu). This will activate your account for Power BI access. Once your password is changed your account should be active at your next login. If you still cannot log in following your password change, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/support) by emailing [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu), by phone at 315.443.2677, or by visiting us at the ITS Service Center. Further information regarding hours and location can be found on the [ITS Service Center](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/) page.

Power BI Desktop is available for download on Windows 7 and newer computers. Detailed instructions can be found in our [Power BI Desktop Installation Guide](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/1oGICQ).

### How do I Manage my Office 365 ProPlus Licenses?

---

[Sign into the Office365 Portal](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/MICICQ#signin)

1. Go to "settings" in the upper right-hand corner (the gear icon next to your name)
2. Select "Office 365 settings"
3. Select "Software"
4. Select "Office"

A list of computers where you have installed Office 365 ProPlus will appear.  Select "deactivate" next to the device to remove the license from that device and make it available for use on another computer.
