Welcome to the home page of IT-related resources at Syracuse University. Below you will find links to pages and websites for academic and enterprise services as well as applications provided by Information Technology Services (ITS) and/or additional schools, colleges, and administrative units throughout campus.

Additional details are available on both the [ITS Student Services](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/RoCICQ) and [ITS Faculty and Staff](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/PoKICQ) Answers pages.

**Teaching and Learning Resources:** Additional teaching and learning resources, including classroom technology support information, is available on the [Teaching and Learning home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/goSICQ).

## Getting Help and Support

If you need technical assistance, ITS support is available anytime at [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) or 315-443-2677 during our [phone support hours](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc/). The ITS Service Center offers [IT related support services](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/z4CICQ) for students on-campus. Further information regarding hours and location can be found on the [ITS Service Center](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/) page.

**Faculty and staff** should contact their [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel for issues related to their University issued or campus managed devices.The ITS Service Center does not have support personnel from your academic or administrative department.

For requesting technology assistance for events, please review the [university events with technology support page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/l4CICQ). Additional resources and information is available on our [Web Conferencing Solutions page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/zIGICQ).

**Want to Learn More?** ITS also provides IT-related [learning opportunities](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/CoKICQ) for some of the systems listed above.
