## NetID Account Guide

Your NetID is your Syracuse University username for campus systems and online services. Use this page to activate your account, manage your password, understand access by affiliation, and find help.

**Manage your account:** [Log in to the NetID Self-Service Portal](https://netid.syr.edu/selfserv/)

### Start here: Activate or manage your NetID

Before you can access university systems such as MySlice, Microsoft 365/SUMail, Blackboard, wireless, or computer labs, you must activate your NetID.

- [Activate your NetID](https://netid.syr.edu/selfserv/acctActivation?execution=e1s1)
- [Manage your NetID or password](https://netid.syr.edu/selfserv/)
- [Review the Password Change FAQ](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/i4CICQ)

### Need SUID help?

Your NetID is different from your 9-digit SUID. For SUID help, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc).

For SUID card assistance, including photo submission, replacement cards, and dependent card requests, visit [Housing, Meal Plans, and I.D. Card Services](https://housingmealplans.syr.edu/i-d-cards/).

## About NetIDs

Access to Syracuse University computing networks and online services is controlled through your NetID username and password.

- Each NetID is automatically created from the user’s name and is unique.
- Applicants receive NetID activation information by email from the Office of Admissions a few business days after submitting an application.
- College of Professional Studies students who are auditing or taking classes as non-matriculated students may receive temporary credentials to get their SUID and activate their NetID. Questions about temporary credentials should be referred to the [Bursar & Registration Office](https://professionalstudies.syracuse.edu/about/office-directory/bursar-registration-office/).
- Faculty and staff receive activation information from their hiring department.
- Parent/Proxy accounts are not NetIDs. Students create and manage Parent/Proxy access for parents or guardians. Learn more about [Parent/Proxy accounts](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/458622318/Proxy+Access+to+MySlice).

## Who is eligible?

NetIDs are automatically created for:

- Applicants for admission to Syracuse University
- Students, as defined by the Registrar
- Faculty and staff, as defined by Human Resources
- Individuals with an eligible sponsorship or association

## Activating your NetID

To access MySlice and other University systems, you must first [activate your NetID](https://netid.syr.edu/selfserv/). Activation requires your SUID, which was included in the Syracuse University email confirming receipt of your application.

### If you cannot find your SUID

Check your spam or junk folder for the confirmation email. If you still cannot locate it, contact Admissions at orange@syr.edu.

**Note:** You will not receive an admission decision through MySlice. You can use MySlice to track missing application materials. Admission decisions are sent by email and may also be followed by mail.

## Password management

All NetID owners are required to change their password at least once per year.

- Email reminders begin 30 days before your password expires.
- If your password expires, your NetID is disabled and you lose access to resources that require your NetID and password.
- More information is available in the [Password Change FAQ](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/i4CICQ).

### Password disabled or expired?

If you know your current password, go to [netid.syr.edu](http://netid.syr.edu), select “Password Expired?”, and use the code sent to the mobile phone or alternate email you set up for recovery.

If you cannot manage your NetID online, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc).

### Forgot your NetID or password?

Start at the [NetID Self-Service page](http://netid.syr.edu/). If you still need help, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc).

### Email support limitations

ITS support staff cannot make changes to user accounts by email request. If you cannot manage your account online, support staff will need to speak with you directly at 315.443.2677.

ITS can accommodate users who are unable to contact support by voice through remote conferencing tools such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Blackboard Collaborate.

## Resource access by affiliation or status

NetID access varies based on your relationship with Syracuse University. Expand the sections below for details.

Applicants may use their NetID to access MySlice. NetIDs assigned to applicants who do not attend the University are disabled one year after the semester for which they applied.

Parents and guardians use a Parent/Proxy account, not a NetID, to access MySlice resources that their student has granted them permission to view.

When applicants become students, access expands to include services such as SUMail, wireless and wired network services, and computer labs. Non-matriculated students receive similar access.

Access duration after a student becomes inactive varies by resource. SUMail and MySlice access are generally retained for about one year after the last term of registration. Data files are retained for one year after the last term of registration if the student’s program is no longer active.

Students under judicial suspension may lose access to email and other University computing services.

**Graduating?** Visit [IT Resource Access for Inactive Affiliations](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940929/IT+Resource+Access+for+Inactive+Affiliations) for complete information.

Faculty and staff use their NetID for resources such as email, wireless, MySlice, computer logins for computers in Syracuse University’s Active Directory domain, and home/shared directories.

Current faculty and staff have access to these resources throughout their employment. Access to certain administrative and academic systems is governed by the data custodians or administrators of those systems.

**Retiring or resigning?** Visit [IT Resource Access for Inactive Affiliations](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940929/IT+Resource+Access+for+Inactive+Affiliations) for complete information.

Alumni retain access to certain systems based on the time elapsed since graduation. For complete information, visit [IT Resource Access for Inactive Affiliations](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940929/IT+Resource+Access+for+Inactive+Affiliations).

Licenses for most IT resources and services are limited to active students, faculty, and staff.

Blackboard uses the NetID and password for authentication, but access to courses, data, and organizations is limited to people enrolled in or administering those specific courses or organizations.

MySlice also uses the NetID and password for authentication; however, access to services within MySlice depends on the person’s University role, position, or student status.

Schools, colleges, and departments may use the NetID and password for specialized systems. Access to those systems is restricted and governed by the relevant unit.

## Changing a NetID

### When ITS may approve a NetID change

Information Technology Services changes NetIDs only when one of the following applies:

1. You have had a legal name change or added a preferred name in MySlice.
2. Your name is misspelled in MySlice.
3. Your current NetID is offensive or inappropriate.

### Before requesting a change

1. If your legal name changed or your name is misspelled, contact Human Resources or the Registrar’s Office to update your official record.
2. Make sure your preferred name is current in MySlice.
3. Be prepared to remain logged out of Syracuse University systems for up to 24 hours on the scheduled change date.

### How to request a NetID change

Email help@syr.edu from your syr.edu email account. ITS will review your request and follow up. Submitting a request does not guarantee that your NetID can be changed.

### New NetID requirements

If approved, ITS support staff will determine an appropriate new NetID. A new NetID:

- Cannot be in use or have ever been used on a centrally administered computer
- Cannot exceed eight characters
- Cannot include non-alphanumeric characters, such as punctuation or symbols
- Must be a reasonable derivation of the client’s name and will be determined by ITS

### Legal name changes

If you recently had a legal name change, consider updating your official University record. See [Personal Data Incorrect in Official University Records](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941498/Personal+Data+Incorrect+in+Official+University+Records) for details.
