## Downloading the App

To download the app, visit either the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kaltura-mediaspace-go/id1054967774) or [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kms.kaltura.kmsapplication&hl=en_US) and search for Kaltura MediaSpace GO.

Users who visit [video.syr.edu](http://video.syr.edu) on a mobile device browser will be prompted to download the KMS GO App.

---

## First Time Setup

Users downloading the app for the first time will need to point their application to the Syracuse University Kaltura environment. To do so, enter '[video.syr.edu](http://video.syr.edu/)' at the initial screen (image below).

## Logging In

Users will need to login once the mobile app is in the Syracuse University Kaltura environment. To log in, first click on the profile symbol in the top left corner on iOS and the top right corner on Android.

**iOS**

![iOS KMS GO home page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941840/IMG_1453.jpeg?api=v2)

**Android**

![Android KMS Go home page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941840/cap2Aedit.png?api=v2)

Next, click on 'Login'.

**iOS**

![Kaltura login screen on iOS](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941840/IMG_1454.png?api=v2)

**Android**

![Kaltura login screen on Android](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941840/Screenshot_20200713-112839.png?api=v2)

You will be prompted to login with your Syracuse University NetID and password.

![syracuse shibboleth login](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941840/cap4.png?api=v2)
