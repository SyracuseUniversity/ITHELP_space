## FAQ

#### **How do I place orders?**

[SHI Catalog Ordering.docx](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/262209636/Staff+and+Faculty+Computer+Procurement?preview=%2F262209636%2F262209663%2FSHI+Catalog+Ordering.docx)

#### **Are bundles available?  What about peripherals and accessories?**

Yes, standard bundles are available via the Company Standards menu.  You can also access a list of Apple, Dell and Lenovo accessories from this menu.

#### **The specific model/config I want isn't listed on the SHI catalog.  How do I order the item?**

Clients are encouraged to select one of the listed standards when possible.  If a special model or config is required, email [syracuse@shi.com](mailto:syracuse@shi.com), listing your desired make, model and config with as much detail as possible.  A quote will be emailed back you.

#### **How are the Company Standards selected?  How can I add or change items?**

A small sub-committee of the Hardware Procurement Project Team meets regularly to review recommended models and specs.  These are chosen to cover the most common procurement scenarios.  To make suggestions or to volunteer to be on the committee, please contact James Warne ([jdwarne@syr.edu](mailto:jdwarne@syr.edu)) or Jeff Pitt ([jdpitt@syr.edu](mailto:jdpitt@syr.edu)).

#### **Will the SHI catalog be integrated into the eProcurement portal?**

Yes, the plan is to add SHI to the list of available vendors.  More information will be shared when this is ready.  For now, the ordering process will involve generating a quote on the SHI catalog, then uploading that quote as a "Special Request" in ePro.

#### **Are laptops and desktops pre-enrolled and configured for Autopilot?**

Yes, by default all laptops and desktops will arrive with Windows 11, will be pre-enrolled with group tag "Assigned", and will be ready for immediate deployment.  The hardware just needs to be unboxed and connected to a wired/wireless network.  Once the primary user logs in, the device will begin configuring itself and installing software.  *If for some reason you do NOT want the device to be pre-enrolled, you must specify this when placing your order.*

#### **Do the Apple devices come pre-enrolled in Apple School Manager?**

Yes.
