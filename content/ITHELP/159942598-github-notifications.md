Notifications can be configured on a per repository, team, or enterprise basis. It is advised to enable a custom routing so that all notifications for repositories, teams, or mentions under the Enterprise will be routed to your @[syr.edu](http://syr.edu) email address. notifications. To configure a custom routing:

Navigate to your [GitHub Settings Notification](https://github.com/settings/notifications) page.

Select the "Custom routing" option.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942598/Screenshot%202023-11-08%20110346.png?api=v2)

Select the "Add new route" option.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942598/Screenshot%202023-11-08%20110601.png?api=v2)

You will be given the option to pick an organization choose the Syracuse University, then select your @[syr.edu](http://syr.edu) email address.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942598/Screenshot%202023-11-08%20110655.png?api=v2)

Now all notifications for repositories, teams, or mentions under the Syracuse University enterprise will be routed to your @[syr.edu](http://syr.edu) email address.
