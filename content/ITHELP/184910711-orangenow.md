# OrangeNow - The official Syracuse University daily companion app

Stay ahead of your day with Syracuse University's OrangeNow. Whether you're navigating your class schedule, catching up on campus news, finding dining options, or checking real-time bus arrivals, OrangeNow has you covered. The OrangeNow app is available for Apple and Android phones and tablets.

For more information, please click on the following page: <https://its.syr.edu/orangenow/>

## Select an Experience

When you first launch the app, you will be prompted to select an experience:

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/184910711/Screenshot%202025-09-12%20at%2010.03.20%E2%80%AFAM.png?api=v2)

Current Syracuse University Students are encouraged to use the **Syracuse University Students** experience. This will prompt you to log in using your netID and password to gain a personalized experience with the following features:

- Access class schedule, view assignment due dates, and get academic support through Blackboard
- View dining center and café menus, hours of operation, and current density
- Learn what is happening on campus with easy access to University events and news
- Seamlessly navigate to key platforms like MySlice, the Patient Portal, SU Mail, and much more!

**All other experiences do not require you to log in and will not have a personalized experience.**
