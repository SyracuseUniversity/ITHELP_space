Your MAC address, or Media Access Control address, is a unique identifier for your machine on a given network, though it has no relation to Apple or Mac computers in title.

# Locating your IP Address and MAC address

1. On your Mac, choose Apple menu   > System Preferences, then click Network.

   ![System preferences window with Network pointed out](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940662/Screen%20Shot%202019-07-16%20at%209.45.49%20AM.png?api=v2)
2. Click the "Advanced..." button.

   ![Network window with advanced circled](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940662/Screen%20Shot%202019-07-16%20at%209.46.19%20AM.png?api=v2)
3. With the menu buttons at the top of the window, you can locate both your IP address and your MAC address.
4. For your IP address, choose the TCP/IP tab.

   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940662/Screen%20Shot%202019-07-16%20at%2010.25.12%20AM.png?api=v2)
5. For your MAC address choose the Hardware tab.
   ![Network hardware page with MAC address highlighted](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940662/Screen%20Shot%202019-07-16%20at%2010.24.52%20AM.png?api=v2)
