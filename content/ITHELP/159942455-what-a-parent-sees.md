Students may elect to share access to their MySlice account with a parent or other interested persons by setting up a proxy account in Share My Access.  Refer to [Proxy Access to MySlice](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/x/bgVWGw) for more information.

The illustration below is an example where the student set up the proxy account to see everything that can be shared.
