## How to Print

There are many ways to print on campus. Use the directions in the sections below for your preferred method.

### **Print from a Campus Computer**

Students can log into any [library](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/f4SGCQ) or [ITS-managed printer](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/h4CICQ)  and print a document.

**Printing Varies by Location**

Some academic departments, such as Maxwell and Newhouse, manage their own labs. To access departmental computer labs, contact the respective academic department or visit the [school and college support page.](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc/dsp/school.html) Those who have provided details instructions regarding printing at their locations can be found [below](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941246/Printing#Printing-departmental).

### **Email to Print**

If you are unsure where on campus you'd like to print, the option of emailing your document to be printed later is enabled. While email to print is enabled, it is limited **black and white duplex printing only**. Be aware that picking any alternative format will have inconsistent results.

**To print**, simply email your document to [print@syr.edu](mailto:print@syr.edu) **from your @**[**syr.edu**](http://syr.edu) **email address**. Students can then log in at any [library](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/f4SGCQ) or [ITS-managed printer](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/h4CICQ) to print their document.

**ITS Lab and Library Printers Only**

Email to print is only available at the library and [ITS-managed printer](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/h4CICQ)s. Guest accounts can utilize email to print using the instructions found on [the Library support page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/display/library/Guests+and+Visitors#GuestsandVisitors-PrintingFromYourPersonalLaptop/MobileDevice).

Once you have sent your email with the document you wish to be printed, log into [printing.syr.edu](http://printing.syr.edu). Your job will populate within 1 minute on the jobs pending release queue.

### **Wireless Printing**

#### Mobility Print

Mobility Print is the preferred wireless printing method for personal laptops and mobile devices in public locations and labs at Syracuse University. 

**For instructions**, select the operating system you would like to print from in the [Mobility Print configuration documentation](https://www.papercut.com/help/manuals/mobility-print/setting-up-a-device-mdns-dns/).

**ITS Lab and Library Printers Only**

Not all printers on campus are available for Mobility Print including printers in office locations and in school or college managed labs. In these cases, you'll be required to manually configure your device to print at your desired location. Manual configuration is available for macOS and Windows computers. Faculty and staff may also contact their [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel for additional options.

**Printing from a personal Mac**

**\*These directions are for personally-owned Macintosh computers connected to the SU network via Ethernet or AirOrangeX.**

**\*You will need to have installed the correct driver of the corresponding printer before adding the printer in System Preferences.**

1. Open *System Settings* and select *Printers and Scanners* from the sidebar.![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941246/Screenshot%202024-08-19%20at%209.21.05%E2%80%AFAM.png?api=v2)
2. Select "**Add Printer**."![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941246/Screenshot%202024-08-21%20at%2012.15.27%E2%80%AFPM.png?api=v2)
3. At the top of **Add Printer** control + click, right click, or two finger click to bring up the sub-menu and select **Customize Toolbar**.
   ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941246/Screenshot%202024-08-21%20at%2012.20.49%E2%80%AFPM.png?api=v2)
4. A new menu will pop up, click and drag the **Advanced** icon to the toolbar, when you see the icon in the tool bar click **Done**.![A screenshot showing the location of advanced icon and an arrow pointing from it to the toolbar as described in this step.](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941246/Screenshot%202024-08-21%20at%201.55.11%E2%80%AFPM.png?api=v2)
5. Click on **Advanced** and select or enter the following information:

   1. **Type:**Windows printer via spoolss
   2. **Device:** Another device
   3. **URL:** enter smb://printers.ad.syr.edu/ followed by the printer name you are trying to add
   4. **Name:**The name should match the URL automatically as it is typed
   5. **Location:** Enter the building and room number where the printer is located e.g. CST 1-227
   6. **Driver:**Select the appropriate driver from the **Select software** option, typing the printer model may find the result you are looking for quicker.
6. If prompted click **Configure** for optional settings such as trays, duplex, memory, etc.
7. Once you hit **OK** your printer should be installed

You are now ready to print!

**Please Note**

When you print for the first time to an SU printer , you will be prompted to authenticate. In the name field enter AD\NetID and your NetID password.

Faculty and staff print jobs are not hold/release, jobs will print immediately.

Students printing to a managed printer via printserver will need to login to [printing.syr.edu](https://printing.syr.edu) with their NetID and password to release their print jobs and be changed against their available Papercut funds.

## Alumni, Retiree and Guest Print Accounts

Alumni, Retiree and Guests of the university are able to print at [public workstations](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/g4SGCQ). Instructions for creating a guest print account can be found [on the alumni and guest printing home](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/gIKICQ).

**Recent Graduate?**

Alumni who have graduated in the last 18 months can still log into [ITS lab locations](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/34CICQ). However, these alumni will still need to create a guest account if they wish to print and will not be able to print using their previous NetID and password login or from funds that existed on their account when they had active status on campus. All users in alumni status will need to print at [public workstations](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/g4SGCQ).

## Personal Wireless Printers

Personal wireless printers are **not permitted** on campus and will not work on University networks. We recommend a Bluetooth or USB cable to connect your printer to your computer instead. Student may request a USB printer cable at the [ITS Service Center](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/).
