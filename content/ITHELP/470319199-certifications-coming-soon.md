**Certifications** are a key part of OrangeAccess and will be introduced in **Summer 2026**.

A certification is a periodic review of access. Managers and application/data owners will be asked to review the access of the people they are responsible for and confirm whether that access is still needed.

---

## Why Certifications Matter

- **Security** – Helps ensure people only keep the access they need.
- **Compliance** – Meets audit and policy requirements for regular access reviews.
- **Accuracy** – Cleans up outdated access when someone changes roles or responsibilities.

---

## What to Expect in 2026

- Managers will receive a list of their employees and their access.
- Application/data owners will receive a list of access tied to their systems.
- For each item, reviewers will choose to:

  - **Certify** – Access is still needed.
  - **Revoke** – Access should be removed.
- OrangeAccess will then update access automatically based on the reviewer’s decisions.

---

✅ **Tip:** You don’t need to do anything now. More information and training will be provided as we get closer to the Summer 2026 rollout.
