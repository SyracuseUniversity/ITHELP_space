## About Microsoft OneNote at Syracuse University

#### What does OneNote provide?

Microsoft **OneNote** is a computer program for free-form information gathering and multi-user collaboration. It gathers users' notes (handwritten or typed), drawings, screen clippings and audio commentaries. Notes can be shared with other **OneNote**users over the Internet or a network.. With OneNote you can:

- Collaborate easily.
- Insert tables or pictures.
- Create text anywhere in the note.
- Collect, store and share unpublished material.
