This page provides information regarding IT resources for former students, faculty and staff at Syracuse University after graduation, retirement, resignation, etc.

## IT Resource Access by Inactive Affiliation

| **Primary Affiliation** | **Affiliation Details** | **General IT Resource Access** | **Limiting Exceptions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alumni and Student Inactive** | **Alumni affiliation** is assigned to former active students who have a graduated and completed a program of study at Syracuse University. This change typically occurs **within 3 months of completion** of the program of study.  **Student Inactive affiliation** is assigned to former SU and ESF active students that have discontinued their program of study and do not also have another completed or active program of study.  **ESF Alumni** are assigned Student Inactive affiliation.  **Former SUPA students** are assigned the student inactive affiliation within 30 days of last academic term. | Former students retain access to IT resources for **12 months after the last term of attendance** including but not limited to:   - SUMail (email) - Microsoft O365 - MySlice - BlackBoard   See [FAQ](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/B4SICQ) for more information about this policy.  Career Services for alumni in Handshake continues in perpetuity.  Please see [Alumni Handshake Accounts](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/SYJqCQ) for more information. | **Claude** - Access to Claude **ends after graduation or leaving the University**. Students will have a period of time of 90 days which begins at the **end date of the last term attended** (example: if the May semester ends 05/05, 90 days starts from there). Students must export all chats or other content before the end of the 90 day period.  **Adobe** - Access to the Adobe Creative Cloud suite and its licensing **ends after graduation or leaving the University**.  **Google Workspace** - Access to the Google Workspace for Education and use of the g.syr.edu for access and authorization to additional Google Services **ends after graduation or leaving the University**. Students will have a period of time of 90 days which begins at the end of last academic term date.  Before the 90 days pass, users can use Google's migration tool, [Google Takeout](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159942679/Google+Takeout), to migrate their data. This process is recommended by Google.  **Printing** - While former students can log into lab machines for a period of time, the printing accounts they had as active users are no longer usable. A guest print account will be needed to print. Instructions to create the account can be found on the [Guest Printing](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/gIKICQ) page.  **Qualtrics -** Further information can be found on the [Qualtrics FAQ Page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/qual/pages/163905655/Qualtrics+SU+FAQ+Page).  **RDS** - Access to the Remote Desktop Service (RDS) is removed when your affiliation is Alumni or Inactive.  **Zoom** - Former students will maintain their Syracuse University Zoom account until 12 months after the last term of attendance; however the account will transition to **a Basic level account** and will no longer have the benefits of a Zoom Edu Pro license. |
| **Staff Inactive and Staff Retired**  **Faculty Inactive**  **ESF Employee Inactive and Retired** | **Staff Inactive or Retired** affiliation is assigned **the day after the effective date of retirement or resignation.**  **Faculty Inactive** affiliation is assigned **the day after the effective date of resignation** by former SU faculty who leave the University for any reason but have **not retired**.  **ESF Employee Inactive and Retired** affiliations fall under these access rules. | SU staff and all ESF employees that resigned or retired and faculty that resigned **do not retain access to IT resources beyond the effective date of retirement or resignation** including but not limited to:   - SUMail (email) - Microsoft O365 - MySlice - BlackBoard | No exceptions. Former staff who have retired or left the University and former faculty who have **not retired** are unable to authenticate to IT resources after leaving the University. |
| **Faculty Retired** | **Faculty Retired** affiliation is assigned to former **SU faculty who have retired,**including Emeritus faculty. | Retired SU faculty **retain access** to**many IT related resources** unless noted.  Faculty retired **email, Microsoft 365, and OneDrive** access is **retained** through yearly password change requirements.  **Note:** Access to some resources, such as **Adobe, Blackboard, and Claude** are based upon the user having had access as an active faculty member. | **Printing** - While retired faculty can log into lab machines, their printing accounts that they used as active users are no longer. A guest print account will be needed. Instructions to create the account can be found on the [Guest Printing](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/gIKICQ) page.  **Google Workspace** - Access to the Google Workspace for Education and use of the [g.syr.edu](http://g.syr.edu) for access and authorization to additional Google Services **ends upon retiring from the University**.  **Zoom** - Retired faculty will maintain their Syracuse University Zoom account, however, the account will be moved to **a 'Basic' level account**. The Zoom Basic account will no longer have the benefits of a Zoom Edu Pro license. |

## Getting Help

#### **Alumni and Inactive Students:**

For support of the information above, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc)  at 315.443.2677, [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu), or by stopping into the ITS Service Center. Further information regarding hours and location can be found on the [ITS Service Center](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/) page.

#### **Former** **Faculty and Staff:**

Many unlisted resources may be provided or supported on a case-by-case or department level. For support of the information above, staff and faculty should first contact their [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel. The ITS Help Desk cannot provide access to these resources.

**Library Emeriti:**
Library Staff elected to Librarian Emeritus status require a temporary sponsorship if retirement precedes board approval of the new status. OAA creates the Emeritus record only after board approval; without a sponsorship bridging that gap, the account is subject to normal lifecycle processes.
