## About Microsoft Power Automate at Syracuse University

#### What does Power Automate provide?

Microsoft Power Automate can be used to create automated workflows between your favorite apps and services. Some examples of what is possible with Power Automate are below.

- Get a notification when you receive an email from a specified sender
- Get notified when a work item is updated

## Accessing Power Automate on Windows or Mac OS

#### [Sign-In to Office 365](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/MICICQ#Office365atSyracuseUniversity-HowdoISign-IntoOffice365andfindavailableOffice365services?signin) - follow Sign-In steps and click on the Power Automate Icon

If you do not see the Power Automate icon, click on "Explore all your apps" and navigate to the Power Automate section.
