Follow the steps below to add a PaperCut printer from a university distributed Windows machine.

# Installing a PaperCut Printer from an SU Owned Windows Machine

1 - (a) Right click **Start**. (b) Select **Control Panel**.

2 - Click **Devices and Printers***.*

3 - Click **Add a Printer**

4 - Click **The printer I want isn’t listed***.*

5 - Click **Add a shared printer by name**

6 - In the name field enter: **\\vpa-papercut-01\**

7 - When the list of printers appears below **\\vpa-papercut-01\**

8 - Select the printer you want to use, then click **Next***.*

9 - On the "You've successfully added" window, do the following*:*

Click **Next***.*

Click **Finish**.
10 - After you have sent a job to be printed, you must visit <http://printing.syr.edu>, logging in with your NETID, to release any jobs to be printed.
