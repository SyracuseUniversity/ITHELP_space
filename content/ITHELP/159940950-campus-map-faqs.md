Below is a list of all the frequently asked questions, or FAQs for the Syracuse University interactive campus map. The more questions that are asked, the more answers we have to help you!

## FAQs

---

### How Do I Access The Map?

Syracuse University's interactive campus map is available at <https://maps.syracuse.edu/>.

### Who Can Use The Map?

Anyone can use the map! Whether you're a student, parent, or just visiting, the map can be used by anyone.

### Where Can I Find Map Documentation?

Links to the documentation can be found under the *Map Documentation* section on the [Syracuse University Interactive Campus Map Support](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941703/Campus+Map+Concept3D#CampusMap(Concept3D)-MapDocumentation) Answers page.

### What If I Can't Find What I'm Looking For In The Documentation?
