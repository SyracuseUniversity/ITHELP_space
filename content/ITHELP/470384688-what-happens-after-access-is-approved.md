When a request is approved in OrangeAccess, it doesn’t always mean access is granted immediately. The timing depends on the system and the type of access requested.

---

## How Access Is Provisioned

- **Automatic provisioning** – For many systems, access is granted automatically once all approvals are complete. This can happen within minutes to a few hours.
- **Batch updates** – Some systems update on a schedule (for example, overnight jobs). In these cases, access may not appear until the next day.
- **Manual fulfillment** – In certain cases, access changes require an administrator to complete the update manually. This may take additional time.

---

## What to Expect by System

- **MySlice (including Campus Solutions, HR/Payroll, and Financials)** – Access is typically available within a few minutes.

  - ⚠️ *Note:* These systems are sensitive to browser cache. If you do not see your new access, try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then log back in.
- **DMC** – Access is typically available within a few minutes.
- **Other applications** – Timing may vary; details will be added as more systems are onboarded to OrangeAccess.

---

## Notifications

- You will receive an email when your request is approved.
- A separate notification is not always sent when the access is fully active. If you do not see the new access after the expected timeframe, check with your manager or submit a oahelp ticket.

---

✅ **Tip:**

- If your access doesn’t appear after one business day, check the **Pending Requests** tile for status updates, or open a ticket in the OT queue [OAHELP](https://support.atlassian.syr.edu/servicedesk/customer/portal/924/group/1213/create/2622)
- For PeopleSoft systems (MySlice, Campus Solutions, HR/Payroll, Financials), **clear your browser cache and cookies** if your new access does not show up after approval.
