You may quickly access frequently used pages by saving them as Favorites in MySlice.  Select the NavBar compass icon (![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942347/Compass.jpg?api=v2)) in the upper-right corner and then select Favorites.  Favorites from all PeopleSoft applications appear in alphabetical order in one list with a prefix to keep them organized by application ("CS" stands for "Campus Solutions; "HCM" stands for "Human Capital Management").

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942347/NavBar%20Favorites%20%5B20230216;%201920x884%5D.jpg?api=v2)

## Add to Favorites

After navigating to the page, select the  3-dots in the upper right corner and select Add to Favorites.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942347/Personalize%20admin%20access.jpg?api=v2)
