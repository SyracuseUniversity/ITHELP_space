# Filtering Logs and Printing Reports

Users in the Admin and Service Center Admin roles can view the logs within their departmental environment. To do so, navigate to the Logs tab.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/joblog.PNG?api=v2)

## Filtering Logs

The default setting for the logs filter will be in the "off" condition, but it may be "on" if previously configured. This will provide an administrative overview of activities on all printers managed by the administrator currently logged in.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/filteroff.PNG?api=v2)

If you'd like to filter the results, click "Filter off" or "Filter on" button, whichever is currently displayed.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/filteroffedit.png?api=v2)

You will now see a multitude of options to filter your log results. If your filter was on previously, click "Clear" to remove it and create a new filter.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/joblogfilter.PNG?api=v2)

Be sure to click Apply Filter.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/applyfilteredit.png?api=v2)

You should now see that the filter is on.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/filteron.PNG?api=v2)

## Export/Print Logs

Administrators with Logs tab access can export or print the default and filtered logs to Excel, pdf, or HTML. To export or print, simply click on the Excel, pdf, or HTML symbols. This allows administrators to produce reports of the default or filters data in the Logs tab. 

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941226/exportprint.PNG?api=v2)
