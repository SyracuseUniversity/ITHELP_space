For the Fall 2022 semester, Syracuse University, in partnership with [JMA Wireless](https://jmawireless.com/), piloted a new Private Cellular Network ([CBRS](https://www.networkworld.com/article/3180615/faq-what-in-the-wireless-world-is-cbrs.html)) in 3 residence halls and a few Academic buildings which provides 5G Cellular connectivity within those areas.  The initial pilot included approximately 1600 First Year students and the Residence Advisors in the 3 locations. The SU Private Network has since grown, and will be extended further across campus in the near future.

***Note: this network is not replacing AOX or other campus Wi-Fi networks, but will work in conjunction with them.***
