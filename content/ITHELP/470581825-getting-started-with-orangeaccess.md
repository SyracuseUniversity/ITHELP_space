OrangeAccess (OA) is Syracuse University’s system for requesting, approving, and reviewing access to applications and data. It replaces the legacy FAST application and provides a modern, secure way to manage access.

---

## Who Uses OrangeAccess?

- **Staff and Faculty** – Request access for themselves or others.
- **Managers** – Approve or request access for their team members.
- **Application/Data Owners** – Approve access for the systems they are responsible for.

---

## Logging In

1. Open a browser and go to [orangeaccess.syr.edu](https://orangeaccess.syr.edu).
2. Log in with your **NetID and password**.
3. After login, you’ll be brought to the **OrangeAccess Home Page**.

📸 *Screenshot:* ![OrangeAccess home page with tiles visible](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/470581825/OA-GettingStarted1.png?api=v2)

---

## Key Features on the Home Page

- **Request Center** – Submit access requests for yourself, your team, or others.
- **Pending Requests** – Track the status of requests you have submitted.
- **Access Requests to Review** – Approve or deny requests assigned to you.
- **My Access** – Review or remove your current access.
- **My Team** – Review or remove access for your direct reports.
- **Preferences** – Manage options like work reassignment.

📸 *Screenshot:* ![Home page showing the available tiles ](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/470581825/OA-GettingStarted1.png?api=v2)

## First Steps for New Users

- Learn how to submit a request, navigate to [[Submit a Request for Myself](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/468091014/How+to+Submit+a+Request+for+Myself)].
- Learn how to review a request, navigate to [[How to Review/Remove My Access](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/469369147/How+to+Review+or+Remove+My+Access)].
- If you are a manager, navigate to [[How to Submit a Request for My Team](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/470024294/How+to+Submit+Request+for+My+Team)] and [[How to Approve a Request](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/469368925/How+to+Approve+a+Request)].
- If you are an application or data owner, navigate to [[How to Remove Access as an Owner](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/470057014/How+to+Remove+Access+as+an+Owner)].

---

✅ **Tip:** OrangeAccess is replacing FAST. Beginning December 3, 2025, all requests for MySlice, Campus Solutions, HR/Payroll, and Financials must be submitted through OA.
