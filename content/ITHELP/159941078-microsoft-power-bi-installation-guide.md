[Power BI](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/en-us/features/) is now included with your Office 365 Pro Plus subscription. Power BI is a cloud-based business analytics service that gives you a single view of your most critical business data. Monitor the health of your business using a live dashboard, create rich interactive reports with [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop) and access your data on the go with native [Power BI Mobile](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/mobile) apps. It’s easy, fast, and free.

**Mac Users**

Unfortunately Microsoft at this time only has Power BI for use on Windows devices. If you are a Mac user and need to use Power BI we would suggest either using the web-based client, an on campus lab computer, or [Remote Desktop Services (RDS)](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941534/Remote+Desktop+Services+RDS).

Faculty and staff should reach out to their [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel.

## Using Power BI Web-based Client

Please use the following steps to use Power BI in your browser of choice.

### 1 - Open a Browser and Locate the Sign On page

Visit  <https://app.powerbi.com/>

### 2 - Sign on Using your SU Email Account Credentials

Log in with your University SUmail account.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941078/Screenshot%202022-11-03%20141740.png?api=v2)

### 3 - Web Platform

Once logged in you should see the Power Bi web platform and you should be able to start using it:

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941078/Screenshot%202022-11-03%20142717.png?api=v2)

### Can't Log In?

If you get the error message below, ensure that you are logging in with your Office 365 ProPlus eligible account. To ensure your account is active please change your NetID password on the [NetID self-service page](http://netid.syr.edu). This will activate your account for Power BI access in the event it was not previously active. Once your password is changed your account should be active at your next login.

 

![PowerBi credential error](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941078/sorrypowerbi2.PNG?api=v2)

If you still cannot log in following your password change, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/support) by emailing [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu), by phone at 315-443-2677.

## Downloading and Installing Power BI Desktop for Windows

Please use the following steps to download and install your copy of Power BI desktop.

### 1 - Open ‘Microsoft Store’ from your computer

Locate Microsoft Store via your computer’s Start Menu and launch it. At the top of the Microsoft Store window, enter ‘Power BI’ in the search box. Choose ‘Power BI Desktop’ from the search results.

![image-20260223-215149.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941078/image-20260223-215149.png?api=v2)

### 2 - Sign on Using your SU Email Account Credentials

With the Power BI Desktop search item open, choose ‘Get’ to install on your device.

![image-20260223-215616.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941078/image-20260223-215616.png?api=v2)

### 3 - Sign on Using your SU Email Account Credentials

After launching Power BI, log in with your University SUmail account from the top right of the application.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941078/9.png?api=v2)

**Congratulations! You've successfully installed Power BI Desktop.**
