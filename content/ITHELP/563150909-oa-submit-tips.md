*✅ Tips:*

- You can always review your full request history under the **Pending Requests** tile, even after requests are complete.
- Managers can request access for **more than one person** at a time by selecting multiple identities.
- Use the **Review Requests** page to double-check that all selected team members and access items are correct before submitting.
- **Bulk uploads or department-wide requests** are not supported — requests must be made for specific individuals.
