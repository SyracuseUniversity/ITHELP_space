**Is this a phish?**

Syracuse University uses email to provide important information regarding account and data retention for former students.  If you have any doubts or concerns about the legitimacy of emails you receive, please contact ITS Client Services at (315) 443-2677 or [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu).

# **Email and OneDrive Changes**

## Why did email and OneDrive access changing for former students and alumni?

Starting in May 2023, The university is updating its policies to ensure the security and efficient management of its resources. This includes managing email accounts and other digital assets.

## When is the last day I will be able to access my emails and files?

**The last day to access your syr.edu account will be 12 months after the end of your last term of enrollment at SU.**

## What will happen to my email and OneDrive files?

Email and files will be deleted soon after access is removed.  It is recommended that you download or transfer important documents and emails before access is removed.

## Will I still be able to access MySlice, BlackBoard and other technology resources?

No.  These resources are available only to current students, faculty and staff, and former students whose last term of enrollment was less than 12 months ago.

## What if I need my SU transcript?

You can order your transcript by following the directions from the [Registrar's Office.](https://registrar.syr.edu/students/transcripts/)

## What can I do to keep my syr.edu email address?

Email and OneDrive services are available to individuals that are actively affiliated with Syracuse University. There are no exceptions.

# **What do I need to do?**

- If you do not already have one, create an email account with another provider such as gmail.
- Download or transfer any important documents and emails from your syr.edu account. You can manually forward messages to another email account.
- Update log-in credentials for websites and applications for which you use your syr.edu email.
- Provide your new email address to Syracuse University’s Office of Alumni Engagement and Annual Giving, visit <https://alumniupdate.syr.edu> to add any new or updated information.

## When do I need to take action?

- Begin transitioning from the syr.edu email account and OneDrive as soon as you graduate or stop attending.
- Access to your [syr.edu](http://syr.edu) and OneDrive will be removed 12 months after the end of your last term of attendance.

## How can I move my email and files?

- Email

  - export to local device.  See instructions at [Microsoft.com](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/import-and-export-outlook-email-contacts-and-calendar-92577192-3881-4502-b79d-c3bbada6c8ef).
  - forward individual messages to a non-syr.edu email account.
- OneDrive

  - do these two steps:

    1. sync to a different device (see instructions at [Microsoft.com](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/sync-onedrive-files-and-folders-d9262485-9bf8-4ceb-bac2-e83f68cb6a97)), and then
    2. copy or move the files to another location out of the SU OneDrive folder.

If you need assistance, contact the ITS Service Center at [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) or 315-443-2677.

How do I give SU my new email address?

Please go to <https://alumniupdate.syr.edu> and add any new or updated information.

# **Continuing or Returning Students**

## I am a former student who is returning to Syracuse University. Will I regain access to my syr.edu account?

Returning students may regain access to their syr.edu accounts if it is less than 12 months since their last class. However, if it has been 12 months or longer, you will still have same email address, NetID, and SUID but any data that was in the account prior will be gone. If you are unsure whether you will be returning before 12 months, it's recommended that you download or transfer any important documents and emails from your syr.edu account before you lose access.

## What if I am not graduating or planning to continue my studies at Syracuse University? Will I still lose my email address?

Students will retain access to their syr.edu accounts 12 after last term of enrollment.  After 12 months you will lose access to email and OneDrive. If you are unsure whether you will return within 12 months, it is recommended that you download or transfer any important documents and emails from your syr.edu account before you lose access.

## What should I do if I have additional questions or need assistance?

For further assistance or clarification regarding the retention policy changes, please feel free to contact the University's IT department directly at (315) 443-2677 or [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu). Our team is available to address any questions or concerns you may have regarding this communication or any other related matters.
