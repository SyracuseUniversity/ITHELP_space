## Private or Incognito

Web browsers contain and save an assortment of information, especially in regards to your passwords and accounts. A private browsing window is a great way to troubleshoot a potential browser issue, or even sometimes get around them if you have issues logging into your email, MySlice, Blackboard, or other pages. Private browser windows do not use or save your information, so if you can't log in this is a quick way for you to get to the bottom of your problem!

Refer to the options below for your browser of choice:

- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/4026200/microsoft-edge-browse-inprivate) Keyboard Ctrl-Shift-N (Windows) or Command-Shift-N (macOS)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/private-browsing-use-firefox-without-history) Keyboard Ctrl-Shift-P (Windows) or Command-Shift-P (macOS)
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en) Keyboard Ctrl-Shift-N (Windows) or Command-Shift-N (macOS)
- [MacOS Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/use-private-browsing-ibrw1069/) Keyboard Command-Shift-N (macOS)
