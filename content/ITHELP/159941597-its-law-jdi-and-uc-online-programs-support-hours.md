## Who We Are and What We Do

Need help with an IT related issue or your campus account? The ITS Service Center offers IT related support to student, faculty, staff, and more 7 days a week. Those seeking assistance can call, email or stop by. Be sure to check out our [full list of in-person support services](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/z4CICQ).

## Hours of Operation

ITS support staff is available to address the IT-related needs of those participating in the Syracuse University Law JDi and University College online programs during the hours below.

For assistance, call **315.443.2677**, email [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu), or [fill out a request form](https://its.syr.edu/create-a-help-ticket/) on the ITS website. For in-person support, please refer to the details and hours on the [ITS Service Center home page](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/).

| **Days** | **Hours** |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday thru Thursday | 8 a.m. to 12 a.m. |
| Friday and Saturday | 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. |
