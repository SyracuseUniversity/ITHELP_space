## 4. Monitor Progress

- The request will follow the standard approval workflow:

  - **Manager approval** (if you are not the employee’s manager).
  - **Application/Data Owner approval** (governance group).
- You can monitor the request status by selecting the **Pending Requests** tile. 📸 *Screenshot:* ![Pending Requests page showing the process card](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/562921490/a9d6615c-fdcb-4c2e-9e7e-d8ef1eafe415?api=v2)
- The employee will also receive an email when their access is approved or denied. 📸 *Screenshot:* ![email sent to employee showing the access decision ](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/562921490/de7077e6-13fd-4d8f-92e8-af33b679f7b0?api=v2)
