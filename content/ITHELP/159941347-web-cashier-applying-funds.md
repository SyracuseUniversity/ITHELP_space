# Applying Funds in Web Cashier

## 1 - Verify Funds

Before proceeding, following the guidelines set by your [organizational unit administrator](http://its.syr.edu/contactus.html) to verify that the funds for the account have been processed properly.

## 2 - Log Into the Web Cashier Interface

Open a web browser and visit printing.syr.edu/webcashier. Login with your administrative account.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/login.PNG?api=v2)

## 3 - Locate the Deposit Tab

Once in the web cashier interface, select the Deposit tab at the top of the screen.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/deposit.PNG?api=v2)

## 4 - Input the Transaction Information

Verify the client's SU NetID and input it into the username field.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/depusnm.png?api=v2)

Enter the amount for the funds being purchased.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/depamnt.png?api=v2)

Select the payment method.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/deppmnt.png?api=v2)

Always Purchased Funds

At the date of implementation, Purchased Funds will be the only option. Additional payment options may become available in the future.

Add a comment to indicate how the client payment was verified prior to beginning the deposit process.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/depcmnt.png?api=v2)

Comments Required

A detailed comment is required for all transactions without exception.

## 5 - Verify Information and Finalize Deposit

Review all data fields before proceeding. After verifying the deposit data is accurate, click Deposit to finalize the transaction.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941347/depdep.png?api=v2)
