Syracuse University provides active faculty, staff, and students access to Listserv. Listserv provides management of mailing lists for groups of users including email newsletters, announcement lists, discussion groups and email communities.

Every user of Syracuse University Listserv should be aware of the [Listserv Policies](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/24KICQ) as well as the [Syracuse University email policy](https://policies.syr.edu/policies/information-technology/e-mail-policy/).

**Need a password?** If you are logging in for the first time or have forgotten your Listserv password, follow the instructions on the [Listserv password page](https://listserv.syr.edu/scripts/wa.exe?GETPW1) to set your password during your first login. If you have forgotten your password it can be reset on the same page. Access to your email address is required. More information about your Syracuse email address can be found on the [Email home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/CIOICQ).

## Getting Help

Subscribers and list members should contact their list owner(s) for assistance by emailing <listname-request@listserv.syr.edu>. For example, if the listserv is clocks@listserv.syr.edu, send email to <clocks-request@listserv.syr.edu>.

For support of the information above, contact the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc) by calling at 315.443.2677, emailing [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu), or visiting the <http://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/> in the Women’s Building.
