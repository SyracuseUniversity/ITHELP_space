Department Managed Printers Only

The steps below will only be available to printers found on the local print server. If a client is requesting a refund for a failed print job from a printer in a location that is not owned by your department, direct them to the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc/).

# Refunding Failed Print Jobs

Users in the Refund Jobs role can perform refunds in the Printers tab within the administrative interface. To do so, navigate to the Printers Tab.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/printertab.PNG?api=v2)

The Printer List sub-tab will be displayed by default.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/printers.PNG?api=v2)

Once there, the steps below will be available.

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941228/Printers+-+Refunding+Failed+Print+Jobs#Printers-RefundingFailedPrintJobs-topofpage)

### 1 - Select the Printer

Confirm with the client the location they attempted to print. Identifying the building and room number is helpful if they do not know the exact printer name. Select the printer in question from the list which will populate automatically.

Department Printers Only

Note that administrators in the Refund Jobs role can not provide refunds for printers outside of the department. If a user is requesting a refund for a printer not listed, please direct them to the [ITS Help Desk](http://its.syr.edu/supportsvc/).

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/printerlists.PNG?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941228/Printers+-+Refunding+Failed+Print+Jobs#Printers-RefundingFailedPrintJobs-topofpage)

### 2 - Locate the Job Log

Once in the printer's details, click on the Job Log tab.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/joblogprinter.PNG?api=v2)

From here, you should see a list of jobs. Note that the default filter will be the last two weeks of activity. 

You can also filter the results based on the information provided by the client. Click "**Filter on**" to see show filter options (or Filter off if previously disabled). Be sure to click "**Apply Filter**" to apply the desired filter.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/printerlogoptions.PNG?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941228/Printers+-+Refunding+Failed+Print+Jobs#Printers-RefundingFailedPrintJobs-topofpage)

### 3 - Identify Failed Print Job

Review the print job to ensure it is the failed print job. You will be able to confirm the date, total pages, cost, and details about the document. 

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/printloginfo.PNG?api=v2)

Pay close attention to the Status column. A refund will be available if the print job has been considered "Printed". If so, click "**refund**" to proceed.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/printloginfo2.png?api=v2)

Refund Not Available

If the refund button is not available the job may have been cancelled indicating the account was not charged. This will be shown as "Cancelled" in this status column.

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941228/Printers+-+Refunding+Failed+Print+Jobs#Printers-RefundingFailedPrintJobs-topofpage)

### 4 - Review Refund Details

To ensure that you are refunding the correct print job, review the information in the Job Details.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/jobdetails2.PNG?api=v2)

[Return to Top](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941228/Printers+-+Refunding+Failed+Print+Jobs#Printers-RefundingFailedPrintJobs-topofpage)

### 5 - Enter Refund Actions and Perform Refund

Confirm the refund amount has translated properly. By default, the cost here will mirror the cost associated with the initial print job.

 ![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/refundactions2.PNG?api=v2)

Provide a detailed comment regarding the nature and reasoning behind the refunded print job. It is recommended that you notate any Orange Tracker ticket number in this area.

 

Comments Required

A detailed comment is required for all transactions without exception.

Once the refund amount is verified and a comment has been inputted, click "OK" to perform the refund.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941228/ok.PNG?api=v2)

The user should immediately see the funds returned to their quota. This action should be visible in their transaction history.
