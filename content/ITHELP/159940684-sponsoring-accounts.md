SU staff and faculty can sponsor two types of accounts:

| Type | Used By | Has SUID | Eligible for ID Card | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SU Guest | For individual users | Yes | Yes | Visiting scholars, visiting students, consultants, volunteers |
| Generic | For multiple users \* | No | No | Departmental email account, student workstudy account, account for emailing an Orange Tracker queue, an account representing a room or a piece of equipment that can be reserved via a netid |

\*In the case of student workstudies, only a single student should be using the generic account at a time, but different students can use the same generic account over time as the employed student changes (ex:  itsworkstudy01).

The sponsor should be someone in the department where the account is used.  The sponsor does not have to have management responsibilities for the person(s) using the account, but should have knowledge of whether the account is still in use.

Responsibilities of the sponsor include:

- Asserting an enabled generic account (ex:  one assigned to a student workstudy) is still in use on a yearly basis by changing the password of the account using netid.syr.edu and giving the password to the appropriate user(s). Note that SU Guests are responsible for maintaining the password on their accounts.
- Changing the password of an enabled generic account when a user of that account changes or leaves SU (don't wait for the yearly password change requirement since the former user will still have the password until it changes).
- Asserting that SU Guest accounts and non-enabled generic accounts (ex:  departmental email account) are still in use and needed by using netid.syr.edu to "re-up" the accounts.
- Notifying their local technical support person when the account is no longer in use so  the sponsorship for SU Guest accounts can be ended (this prevents the account and ID card from being used)  or the generic account can be decommissioned  (decomissioning a generic account does not remove it – it treats it as an inactive account).
- If the sponsor changes positions or leaves SU, they should change the sponsor of the account (using netid.syr.edu) before their job end date to someone else in the department who can manage the account going forward.

For more information on how to change the password of a generic account, change the sponsor of a generic account, and/or "re-up" a generic or SU Guest account, see [Managing Sponsored Accounts Using netid.syr.edu](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159942313/Managing+Sponsored+Accounts+Using+netid.syr.edu).

To request an "SU Guest" or generic account, sponsors should contact their local technical support unit.  Technical support units have the ability to initially set up the SU Guest account or generic account.  The sponsor's SUID will be associated with the account, and the sponsor will receive yearly emails as reminders to change the password of the account or "re-up" it to assert that it is still active, depending on the account type and usage.
