## About SUMail Accounts

SUMail is managed by the University and utilizes Microsoft's Cloud services. This partnership provides students several powerful and convenient features, including:

- Increased mailbox sizes up to 100 GB
- Modern Outlook Web experience
- Increased security with Multi-Factor Authentication
- Increased spam, virus, and phishing protection

Your SUMail email address is your [NetID@syr.edu](mailto:NetID@syr.edu). For example, if your NetID is “**xyzsmith**”,your SUMail email address would be **xzysmith**@syr.edu.

University affiliates that qualify for SUMail (such as students, faculty, staff, and sponsored associates) are expected to use it as the primary source of communication. Effective March 2025, automatic forwarding of SUMail to 3rd party email accounts is not available.

**SUMail uses the same password that you set up for your NetID.**

To manage your NetID, including password management, activation, and multi-factor authentication (MFA), visit <http://netid.syr.edu>.

To add your SUMail to a personal device, use the instructions on the [SUMail Configurations page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/JYWICQ).

Want to filter mail, file junk, or block messages? SUMail accounts can utilize the Junk folder to both manage mail filtering and block messages. Additional information, including instructions, can be found on the [Microsoft Junk Folder](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/1YaICQ) page.

### New Student? When can you log in?

Newly matriculated students are assigned an official email address approximately 24-48 hours after completing the following:

- Submit the Admissions Acceptance Form (undergraduate) or the Intent to Register Form (graduate, Law)
- Paid Tuition Deposit
- Are Confirmed as Matriculated
- Activated their NetID

First-time non-matriculated students (e.g. University College) are assigned an official email address usually within 24 hours after submitting their registration.

## SUMail Access After Graduation, Resignation, or Retirement

Syracuse University licenses email accounts for active students, staff, faculty, retired faculty (including emeriti), and sponsored associates whose role supports the University's mission. When a person's role is no longer active, access to the mailbox will be removed and data will be deleted.

For complete information, including how long you will have access after your status change, please review our page on [IT Resource Access for Inactive Affiliations](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/QYGICQ).
