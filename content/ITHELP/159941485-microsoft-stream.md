## About Microsoft Stream at Syracuse University

#### What does Stream provide?

Microsoft Stream can be used to upload, view, and share videos securely. You can share recordings of classes, meetings, presentations, training sessions, or other videos that aid your team's collaboration.

Microsoft Stream also makes it easy to share comments on a video, tag timecodes in comments and descriptions to refer to specific points in a video and discuss with colleagues.

## Accessing Stream on Windows or Mac OS

#### [Sign-In to Office 365](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/MICICQ#signin) - follow Sign-In steps and click on the Stream Icon

If you do not see the Stream icon, click on "Explore all your apps" and navigate to the Stream section.

## Accessing Stream on Mobile Devices

Microsoft Stream is available on mobile browsers with full functionality across all platforms (iOS, Android,
Windows Phone).

#### [Add subtitles or caption to your videos](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/stream/portal-add-subtitles-captions)
