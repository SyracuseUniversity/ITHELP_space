## Download and Install Outlook

Visit the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.office.outlook&hl=en_US) to download and install Outlook.

![Install button on Google Play Store](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/1%20Outlook%20install%20from%20Google%20Play.png?api=v2)

## Configuring the Outlook app for **first time** users

1. Once you have installed the Outlook app, open it.
2. Click on **Add Account**.

   ![add account blue button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/2%20add%20account.png?api=v2)
3. On the 'Add Account' screen, enter your **full email address** (e.g. NetID@syr.edu)

   ![Add account screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/3%20enter%20netid.png?api=v2)
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Enter your NetID password on the Office 365 sign in page and select **Sign In**.
        a. Subsequently, you will also need to verify your identity with your two-factor authentication method (MFA/2FA).

   ![Office 365 password page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/4%20enter%20password.png?api=v2)![2FA Code Page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/5%202fa.png?api=v2)
6. Your account will be added to the Outlook app.

## Removing an email account from Outlook

1. Launch the Outlook app.
2. At the **top left,** select the **menu icon**, tap **Settings**.

   ![setting icon in pull out tray](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/6%20settings%20icon.png?api=v2)
3. Tap the account you would like to remove.

   ![Mail accounts screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/7%20setting%20screen.png?api=v2)
4. A new window will appear. Tap **Delete Account**.
    
   ![delete account screenshot](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/8%20email%20account%20with%20delete.png?api=v2)
5. A small menu will appear, tap **Delete**.

   ![secondary delete button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/9%20double%20delete.png?api=v2)
6. Fully quit the application.

## Adding an email account to Outlook

1. Launch the Outlook app.
2. At the **top left,** select the **menu icon**, tap **Settings**.

   ![setting icon in pull out tray](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/6%20settings%20icon.png?api=v2)
3. On the 'Add Account' screen, enter your **full email address** (e.g. [NetID@syr.edu](mailto:NetID@syr.edu))

   ![Add account screen](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/3%20enter%20netid.png?api=v2)
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Enter your NetID password on the Office 365 sign in page and select**Sign In**. 
        a. Subsequently, you will also need to verify your identity with your two-factor authentication method (MFA/2FA).

   ![Office 365 password page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/4%20enter%20password.png?api=v2)![2FA Code Page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940717/5%202fa.png?api=v2)
6. Your account will be added to the Outlook app.

## Additional set-up instructions for native mail apps

As a wealth of technology continues to wash over us all, there continues to be many nuances in native mail applications. Due to this ever changing landscape, we recommend reviewing the settings/instructions for how to use your device specific mail app on device manufacturer's website.

- [Samsung mail app](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS00078934/)
- [Lenovo mail app](https://support.lenovo.com/us/en/solutions/ht100023-setting-up-an-email-account-smartphonetablet)
- [LG mail app](https://www.lg.com/us/mobile-phones/VS985/Userguide/243.html)
- [Setting up email in an Android app](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/set-up-email-in-android-email-app-71147974-7aca-491b-978a-ab15e360434c)
- [Google Pixel devices](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/2840815?hl=en)
