**Microsoft 365 is about productivity**

Microsoft 365 is a suite of productivity tools that enables you to work more efficiently and effectively. It includes classic Microsoft Office applications like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, as well as other secure productivity services that are delivered over the Internet.

With Microsoft 365, you can store and share files with others using OneDrive, collaborate with colleagues in real-time using Teams, and create digital notebooks with OneNote. These tools allow you to communicate and collaborate with others, even when you're not in the same location. You can access your files and applications from anywhere and on any device, making it easy to stay productive whether you're on campus or on the go.

### Logging in to Microsoft 365

---

To access Microsoft 365 at Syracuse University, follow these steps:

1. Go to [office.syr.edu](http://office.syr.edu).
2. Enter your [NetID@syr.edu](mailto:NetID@syr.edu) email address and NetID password.
3. Click on the "Sign In" button.

### Email Access

Syracuse University uses email as the primary vehicle for official communication with students, staff, and faculty. Complete details, including login links, are available on the [SUMail email home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/CIOICQ).

### Downloading the Office applications

---

Installable Office applications are available for active students and full time staff.

To download the Office applications, go to [office.syr.edu](http://office.syr.edu) and follow these steps:

1. Click on the square made of 9 dots at the top-left of the screen.

![An arrow pointing to the 9 dots icon](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940656/1.png?api=v2)

2. Then, click on the “More apps” button.

![A screenshot of an arrow pointing to the More apps button](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940656/2.png?api=v2)

3. Next, click on “Install apps” on the top-right corner, and then click on “Microsoft 365 apps”.

![A screenshot showing Install Apps and Microsoft 365 apps circled](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940656/3.png?api=v2)

4. On the following page, click “Install Office” under the Apps & devices > Office. This will download the installer file which you can open after it downloads to install the Microsoft 365 apps.

![The apps and devices page with an arrow pointing to Install Office](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940656/4.png?api=v2)

5. Please start your computer after you install, and when you open any Microsoft 365 apps, you will be prompted to sign in with your NetID/SUmail account info.

Note: You can also access Microsoft 365 web apps and services by clicking on the desired app from the “Apps” section from Step 3.

### Features and Included Applications

---

Microsoft 365 includes a range of applications and services to help you work more productively and collaboratively. Some of the most popular features include:

- [OneDrive](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159940660/Microsoft+OneDrive): Store and share files securely in the cloud.
- [Teams](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941055/Microsoft+Teams): Collaborate with colleagues in real-time through chat, video calls, and document sharing.
- [OneNote](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941057/Microsoft+OneNote): Create digital notebooks to organize your notes and ideas.
- [Forms](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941424/Microsoft+Forms): Create surveys, quizzes, and polls to gather feedback and data.
- [Power Automate](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941419/Microsoft+Power+Automate): Automate tasks and workflows to save time and streamline processes.
- [Planner](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941430/Microsoft+Planner): Organize tasks and projects with visual, interactive boards.
- [Stream](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941485/Microsoft+Stream): Share and watch videos securely within your organization.
- [Power BI](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941078/Microsoft+Power+BI+Installation+Guide): Create dynamic, data-driven dashboards and reports.
- [Windows 10 Education](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/ITHELP/pages/159941446/Microsoft+Windows): Access the latest version of the Windows operating system for education.
- Downloadable Microsoft Office Applications: Access classic Office applications like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint on your desktop or mobile device.

### What if I am No Longer a Current Student or Staff/Faculty?

---

If you are no longer eligible for the Microsoft Licensing Program that provides access to Microsoft 365, you may need to purchase a personal license through Microsoft or seek out other commercial or consumer Microsoft 365 plans.

Keep in mind that once your eligibility ends, your access to Microsoft 365 services may be limited or discontinued, so it's important to retrieve any personal data you need to retain before leaving the University.

Refer to the  [IT Resource Access for Inactive Affiliations](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/QYGICQ) page for information about access to Microsoft 365 after graduation or retirement.

### Microsoft 365 Add-ons

---

While add-ons can provide additional functionality and convenience to the Office suite, Syracuse University's license for Microsoft 365 does not currently allow for add-ons to be added or supported by ITS or end-users.

If you would like to use Microsoft 365 with add-ons, you will need to purchase a personal license and add them at your own discretion.

Faculty and staff with questions about add-ons on Syracuse University-issued devices should contact their [academic](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) or [administrative](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/departmental-support-contact-information/) support personnel.
