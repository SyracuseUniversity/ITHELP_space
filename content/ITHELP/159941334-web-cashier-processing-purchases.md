# Process a Purchase in Web Cashier

## 1 - Verify Client Purchase

Before proceeding, following the guidelines set by your [organizational unit administrator](http://its.syr.edu/contactus.html) to verify that the funds for the account have been processed properly.

## 2 - Log Into the Web Cashier Interface

Open a web browser and visit [printing.syr.edu/webcashier](http://printing.syr.edu/webcashier). Login with your administrative account.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941334/login.PNG?api=v2)

## 3 - Locate the Purchase Tab

Once in the web cashier interface, you should be on the Purchase tab. If not, select the Purchase tab at the top of the screen.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941334/purchase.PNG?api=v2)

## 4 - Input the Transaction Information

Verify the client's SU NetID and input it into the username field.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941334/purchaseusrnm.png?api=v2)

After selecting the user, their current cumulative quota amount will be listed.

Does Not Itemize

Note that the quota amount listed here combines both Quota and Purchased Funds purses. The client must be aware of their Purchased Funds available amount as Web Cashiers are unable to print using the Quota purse funds.

Select Personal account and then select Purchased Funds (added funds) from the drop down menu.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941334/purchasepurse.PNG?api=v2)

Shared Account

There is currently no support for shared accounts.

Under the Item area, select the type of print job the client would like. Be sure to list the property quantity and review the cost associated with the client.

Department Specific

The listed job types will be limited by your organizational unit administrator and are limited to five per organizational unit. If you are seeking an option that is not listed, please contact your [organizational unit administrator](http://its.syr.edu/contactus.html).

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941334/purchaseitem.png?api=v2)

Add a comment regarding the purchase as requested by the client.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159941334/purchasecmnt.png?api=v2)

Comments Required

A detailed comment is required for all transactions without exception.

## 5 - Verify Transaction Information and Finalize Purchase

Review each data field before proceeding. After verifying the purchase data is accurate and confirming with the client, click Purchase to finalize the transaction.

## Web Cashier Refunds

If you have processed a purchase through the Web Cashier and need to refund the transaction, please contact the [ITS Service Center](http://www.its.syr.edu/supportsvc). To do so, email the details of the print job to [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu.) with the subject "Web Cashier Refund". Please include the client's NetID, the date/time/location of the print job (if applicable), and the reason for the refund.

OU Admin, Web Cashier and Refund Job Administrators Only

The ITS Service Center can only process refunds for Web Cashier purchases that have been requested by administrators in the OU Admin, Web Cashier, or Refund Jobs role. Requests made by any other user will result in the requester being redirected to the appropriate organizational unit administrators.
