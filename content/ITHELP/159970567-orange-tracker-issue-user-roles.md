Each ticket can have multiple people involved. Most times you will have just a Reporter and an Assignee. Here are the people you might see:

- **Assignee**: The person tasked with resolving the issue.
- **Reporter**: The customer who sent the request.
- **Request participants** and **Organizations**: Customers and groups of customers who can view and comment on the issue. This is a new field that will be useful.
- **Votes**: People who vote for an issue are people who want the issue resolved.
- **Watchers**: Team members in your project who receive notifications about the issue.
- **Approvers**:If the issue has approvers, this field displays people who are tasked with approving or declining the request.
