Many schools and colleges at Syracuse University have additional information related to resources and support provided specifically for their faculty, staff, and students. Below you'll find links each school and college's websites, Answers space (if available), as well as contact information for their IT services.

**Need IT support?** Contact methods and operating hours for IT personnel in each school & college are available on the [academic IT department contact page](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/).

**Looking for an Administrative Department?**

Administrative department specific information and Answers spaces can be accessed through the [Campus Departments page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/iYWICQ).

**School and College Value Propositions**

Value propositions aligned to the overall Syracuse University brand strategy are available on the [School & College Brand Value Propositions page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/KUaPCQ).
