If your department or unit needs a new application integrated into OrangeAccess (OA) for access requests and approvals, submit a request using the **New Application Request Form**.

---

## Step 1: Submit the Request

1. Open the Jira form: **[New Application Request Form]** (link to your Jira form).
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. Submit the form. The request will be created in the **OAHELP** Jira queue and assigned to the Identity & Access Management (IAM) team.

📸 *Screenshot: Example Jira request form.*

---

## Step 2: Review by IAM Team

- The IAM team will review the submitted details.
- They may contact you for additional information or clarification.
- You will receive Jira updates as the request moves through the workflow.

---

## Step 3: Implementation

- If approved, the IAM team will schedule the onboarding of the application into OrangeAccess.
- You will be notified when the application is live and available in OA.

---

✅ **Tip:** Submitting complete information in the form will help us process your request more quickly.
