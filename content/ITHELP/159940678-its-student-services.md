Welcome to computing at Syracuse University. Be sure to refer to our [ITS New Student Welcome page](https://its.syr.edu/welcome/) as you prepare to arrive on campus.

Syracuse University's Information Technology Services ([ITS](http://its.syr.edu/)) provides students, faculty, and staff with technology resources and services that support the University's mission of promoting learning through teaching, research, scholarship, creative accomplishment and service. ITS works in partnership with the technology departments located within SU's schools, colleges and administrative divisions. Additional links and support information for even more services is available on our [Services and Applications](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/s4KICQ) page.

## IT Support Services

Information Technology Services (ITS) provides a variety of support options for students, faculty, and staff. Additional computing support services are provided by school, college, and departmental computing support staff.

### **Phone Support**

![A graphic art of a phone is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/phone.PNG?api=v2)

Need help? Call 315.443.2677. Our IT consultants will review any issues related to your campus account(s), general computing, or any other questions you may have.

[Hours of Operation](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/)

**Note:** For more efficient service, please have your SUID or NetID available so we may properly identify the account(s) in question.

### **Email Support**

![Email logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/email.PNG?api=v2)

Whether it's an IT related issue or you simply have an inquiry, send an email to [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) 24/7 and the next available consultant will assist you or connect you with the proper IT personnel or department.

**Note:** Never include sensitive personal information such as your password(s) or social security number. Help requests requiring full time staff verifying this information will require a phone interaction or ITS Service Center visit.

### **In-Person Support**

![A graphic art of a people talking is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/inperson-support.png?api=v2)

Open 7 days a week, the ITS Service Center's hours and location can be found on the [ITS Service Center](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/) page. Here we offer a [wide variety of services](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/z4CICQ), including software support, virus removal, diagnostics, and troubleshooting for all students. Be sure check our [supported devices list](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/zYCICQ), which includes the most commonly owned devices and operating systems of our student. [Hours of Operation](https://its.syr.edu/its_service_center/)

Got that DIY spirit? Rather get hands-on with your repair than give it to somebody else? We've got tools for that. Work space, too. And we'd be happy to let you borrow both through our U-FixIT Program.

### **Password Management**

![A graphic art of a lock is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/password.png?api=v2)

Visit our [NetID Self Serv](https://netid.syr.edu) page to activate your NetID or to manage your password. This password is used throughout campus to log into your campus resources including [MySlice](http://myslice.syr.edu), [SUmail](http://sumail.syr.edu) and [Blackboard](http://blackboard.syr.edu).

Students, Staff, and Faculty need to change their SU NetID password at least once a year.

**Note:** Parent/Proxy account passwords are set/reset by the student and are not affected by this procedure. Follow our detailed instructions for [setting up parent/proxy account access](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/2oCICQ).

### **IT Accessibility at SU**

![Accessibility logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/access.png?api=v2)

Information Technology and Services (ITS) offers a variety of resources for using technology to ensure accessibility for all members of the Syracuse University community. [Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Accessibility](http://its.syr.edu/accessibility/index.html) at SU

means ensuring that all technology is usable by the greatest number of students, faculty and staff possible, including those with disabilities, so that everyone is able to participate to the maximum extent that they can.

ITS provides tools and advice for making your digital documents accessible. Check out the [Accessible Communications Toolkit](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/j4CICQ)  for details.

### **IT Security**

![A graphic art of a computer screen with a shield icon is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/security.png?api=v2)

Protecting your information and devices from unauthorized access and data theft requires your vigilance, especially with the evolving nature of cybercrime.

Visit [Information Security home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/AgB8CQ) for more information about IT security at the University.

---

**Protect Yourself!**

Don't share or reuse your passwords. Nobody at SU, including ITS, will ever ask for your password. Do not respond to any requests to reveal your password or other private information, including online and email requests. Visit <https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/x/AgB8CQ> for advice on making and remembering strong passwords, keeping your devices safe, and other information security tips and techniques

## World-class Technology At Your Fingertips - Additional IT Resources and Services Provided

### **AirOrangeX**

![Wi-Fi symbol](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/wifi.PNG?api=v2)

AirOrangeX provides access to Syracuse University's high speed wireless network. To access the network simply click on the network or choose the network from the utility that your device uses to connect to a wireless network. No login is required. This network can be used by laptops, smart phones, games and most devices with wireless connectivity.

### **Student Email**

![A graphic art of a computer and document is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/docs-o365.png?api=v2)

Students have access to Outlook email accounts accessed on our [SUmail page](http://sumail.syr.edu) or with a supported email application.

When you graduate or leave the university, your access to SU email will only be available for 12 months after your last active academic term. After this period access to the account will be removed and the information will no longer be accessible. This account should be used for school related purposes as continued access in the future is limited.

### **Microsoft 365**

![Microsoft 365 (2022) Logo PNG Vector](https://seeklogo.com/images/M/microsoft-365-2022-logo-7B23759A49-seeklogo.com.png)

Syracuse University established an organizational account on the Microsoft 365 platform. This organizational account provides Syracuse University’s students, faculty, and staff with a wide array of cloud-based services and provides new tools for collaboration, teaching, and learning.

Licenses provided to students allow for installation and use of select Microsoft 365 applications on personal devices including online storage with OneDrive.

Tools can be accessed by following the instructions on the [Microsoft 365 at Syracuse University home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/MICICQ).

### **Computer Labs**

![A graphic art of a computer is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/labs.PNG?api=v2)

ITS has [public computer labs](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/34CICQ) conveniently located around campus for students to use. The computers are directly connected to the SU campus network, allowing e-mail transactions and Internet access.

A full list of software available at every ITS-managed computer lab is available on [the public computer labs page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/34CICQ).

Some academic departments, such as Maxwell and Newhouse, manage their own labs. To access departmental computer labs, contact the respective academic department or visit [School and College Support](https://its.syr.edu/contact_its/school-and-college-support-contact-information/) page.

**Note:** In order to use any of the computers within the labs, you must have a valid NetID and password.

### **Remote Desktop Services (RDS)**

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/RDS_SU_Icon-01.png?api=v2)

Syracuse University's **Remote Desktop Services** (RDS) is a virtual desktop environment for students, faculty, and staff to remotely access a university desktop from any personal computer with a stable broadband internet connection.

Complete details, including configuration instructions for both web interface and via a remote desktop (RDP) connection, are available on the [Syracuse University Remote Desktop Services page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/noOICQ). If you know how to connect already, feel free to go directly to [rds.syr.edu](https://rds.syr.edu/).  

### **Printing on Campus**

![A graphic art of a printer is shown](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/printer.PNG?api=v2)

Students are provided a $40 credit at the beginning of each academic year in August (this $40 credit covers the next 12 months) for printing services in the [ITS public computer labs](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/34CICQ).

The University's online [Print Quota Management System](http://printing.syr.edu/) automatically tracks the activity of your public printing.

Students can use their print quota at the ITS-managed printers. Find more information on our [Printing at Syracuse University home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/foKICQ).

### **MakerSpace**

![Black geometric shapes forming the letters M and S](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/makerspace.PNG?api=v2)

Put your hands on desktop computers equipped with high-end software for any needs. Output to laser printers, 3D-printers, and other tools. If you can dream it, you can do it here at the [MakerSpace](http://makerspace.syr.edu/).

The SU MakerSpace encourages students to utilize the space for collaborative educational projects. For more information or to request a tour of the SU MakerSpace please [fill out a contact form](https://makerspace.syr.edu/contact/) or call 315-443-2677.

### **Google Workspace**

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/google%20workspace.png?api=v2)

Syracuse University established an organizational account in Google Workspace. This organizational account provides Syracuse University’s students, faculty, and staff with a wide array of Google cloud-based services and provides new tools for collaboration, teaching, and learning.

A list of Google services, account information, and products accessible through these user accounts is provided on the [Google Workspace at Syracuse University home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/LICICQ).

Note that Syracuse Google accounts are in the format of NetID@g.syr.edu

When you graduate or leave the university, your access to Google drive and other Google services will only be available for 90 days after your last active academic term. After this period access to the account will be removed and the information will no longer be accessible. This account should be used for school related purposes as continued access in the future is limited.

## Educational Technologies

ITS offers students access to the latest in educational technology. These resources provide support in the appropriate use of technology for learning and research. This is just a short list, for a full list of learning tools visit <https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/blackboard01/overview#Campus-Partner-Managed-Tools>

![blackboard learn ultra logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/blackboardu.PNG?api=v2)

[Blackboard](https://blackboard.syr.edu) learning management software makes class content available to students via the Internet. Blackboard provides students and instructors with interactive web 2.0 technologies such as blogs and wikis. The Blackboard course management platform also supports video and audio from a variety of web sources including podcasts and v-casts. The Blackboard course management system uses all of these tools to enhance learning, interaction, and collaboration.

Check out our [introductory video](https://youtu.be/8mqBLHBaPoU) to get started!
 

![Zoom Video Conferencing logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/zoom-logo.png?api=v2)

[Zoom](http://zoom.syr.edu/) is a real-time video conferencing tool that allows users to engage, educate, and collaborate with HD-quality video and audio.  Please view the [Zoom at Syracuse FAQ](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/qoOICQ) for more more information about Syracuse University Zoom.

![Kaltura logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/kaltura-logo-nrml.png?api=v2)

The [Kaltura](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/EIWICQ) Video Platform simplifies video capture, management, and distribution. Kaltura allows instructors and learners to add or embed rich media content to their courses including in assignments, discussions, and more. 

![LinkedIn Learning Logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/LinkedInLearningStackedFullColorLogo.png?api=v2)

Experience the benefits of LinkedIn Learning. LinkedIn Learning provides access high quality on-demand courses. And you’ll get personalized course recommendations based on unique, data-driven insights from millions of professionals on the worldwide LinkedIn network.

Active faculty, staff, and students can log into LinkedIn learning by visiting [linkedinlearning.syr.edu](https://linkedinlearning.syr.edu/) and logging in with their NetID and password managed at [netid.syr.edu](https://netid.syr.edu/).

More information, including a full user's guide provided by LinkedIn Learning, can be found on the [LinkedIn Learning at Syracuse University home page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/SIOICQ).

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/readwrite.PNG?api=v2)

[Read&Write Gold](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/_guACQ) helps all individuals succeed regardless of ability or learning style.  Users can access the reading, writing, studying, and research tools they need at school, home, or work. Having text read aloud with dual color highlighting, along with additional support tools, helps every learner gain confidence and work independently at their own pace.

Syracuse University maintains a site license for TextHelp Read&Write Gold for PC and Mac and iReadWrite for iPad. The software will soon be installed on the computers in the [ITS public computer labs](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/34CICQ) and can now be installed on users’ University- and individually-owned computers.

![Poll Everywhere Logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/unnamed.png?api=v2)

[Poll Everywhere](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/x/EYEbFg) is a web-based tool for making presentations interactive. Instructors can engage students, gauge their understanding, and get feedback in real time. It can be used inside or outside of Blackboard.

![TurnItIn Text Logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/Turnitin.jpg?api=v2)

The [Turnitin](http://turnitin.com/) internet-based plagiarism-detection service checks the originality of student submitted essays. Turnitin can be used to detect plagiarism as well as spelling and use of grammar.  Instructors can leave a voice comment with each submitted assignment. Students and instructors can use Turnitin within the Blackboard platform. Turnitin can be access from the building block in [Blackboard](http://blackboard.syr.edu).

![Qualtrics Logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/qualtric.PNG?api=v2)

[Qualtrics](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/AgDFCQ) provides a unique cloud based research/survey tool licensed for all current SU faculty, Students and Staff.

Qualtrics is one of the very few survey providers that gives unlimited support to all users.

[Get Started Now!](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/VwDFCQ)

**Note:** Qualtrics is licensed for all current SU faculty, Students and Staff. Login is achieved through a secure process in which your credentials are never given to Qualtrics.

![A picture of the WeVideo logo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159940678/WeVideo%20Logo%20-%20flat.png?api=v2)

[WeVideo](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/x/rckzCQ) is an interactive video platform that allows educators to embed elements such as quiz questions or reflection prompts into videos from YouTube, Vimeo, or Kaltura, to enhance student engagement and learning.
