As part of Syracuse University's ongoing efforts to maintain and secure email services, SUMail account holders are no longer allowed to automatically forward or redirect emails to third-party accounts. Note that the SU Policy pages have not yet been updated to reflect this change.

Rules that automatically forward email from @syr.edu to another email system will be deleted by SUMail administrators in March 2025.

You can remove automatic forwarding from your SUMail inbox by following these instructions.

1. Visit <http://sumail.syr.edu> and login using your SUMail address and NetID password.

![step1.PNG](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/262242675/step1.PNG?api=v2)

2. Select the *gear icon* on the top right-hand corner of the webpage. This will open up a submenu. Within this submenu, please click on the search bar that reads *Search settings*.

![step2marked.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/262242675/step2marked.png?api=v2)![step3marked.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/262242675/step3marked.png?api=v2)

3. Type *Forwarding* in the search bar. Click *Forwarding* from the submenu once it becomes available.

![step4marked.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/262242675/step4marked.png?api=v2)

4. Toggle the *Enable Forwarding* option *Off* to disable forwarding.

![step5marked.png](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/262242675/step5marked.png?api=v2)

If you require any assistance while following the steps above, please reach out to the ITS Help Desk at 315-443-2677, or at [help@syr.edu](mailto:help@syr.edu) for email support.
