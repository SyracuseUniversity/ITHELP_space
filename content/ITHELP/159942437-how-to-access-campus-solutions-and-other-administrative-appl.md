Campus Solutions and other administrative applications (Financials, HCM) are available in the Navigator.

1. Select the NavBar compass icon (![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942347/Compass.jpg?api=v2)) in the upper-right corner of the page.
2. Then select the Menu icon.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942437/Administrative%20Applications%20%5B20230216;%20943x805%5D.JPG?api=v2)

Select "Campus Solutions" to expand the menu.

![](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/download/attachments/159942437/Campus%20Solutions%20Menu%20%5B20230216;%20943x805%5D.JPG?api=v2)
