# Welcome to Kaltura at Syracuse University!

Kaltura can be used to publish video directly in our video portal, embed video in webpages you house, or posted in Blackboard by instructors and students.

## [Kaltura Video Portal Support](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/iISICQ)

The [video portal at video.syr.edu](https://video.syr.edu) allows all active faculty, staff, and students to host and share video directly from the portal or embed it in other websites you author.

For instructions, tutorials, and how-to documentation related to the web interface, please refer to the [Kaltura VideoPortal Answers page](https://su-jsm.atlassian.net/wiki/x/iISICQ).

## [Kaltura in Blackboard](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/blackboard01/pages/154390231/Kaltura+Video)

Kaltura is the preferred platform for academic and instructional video and is integrated with Blackboard for posting video in courses.

For instructions, tutorials, and how-to documentation related to Kaltura in Blackboard, please refer to the [Kaltura in Blackboard support page](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/blackboard01/pages/154390231/Kaltura+Video).
